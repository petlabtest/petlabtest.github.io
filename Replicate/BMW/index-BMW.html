ABOUT THE FINANCIAL YEAR

Walter
Mertl
Member of the Board of Management of BMW AG, Finance

In 2025, the BMW Group once again demonstrated its resilience and strategic consistency in a challenging global environment. Tariffs, currency developments and intense competition – particularly in China – shaped the year and demanded both flexibility and discipline from our organisation.

Thanks to our strong global footprint and our attractive product portfolio, nearly 2.5 million customers worldwide chose a vehicle from the BMW Group. Our electrified vehicles are a foundational pillar in our strategy, with more than one in four vehicles delivered being either all-electric or a plug-in hybrid. In Europe, our fleet CO2 emissions once again outperformed regulatory requirements – underscoring our commitment to sustainable mobility.

Financially, we delivered solid results. The Automotive segment achieved an EBIT margin within our target range, and at Group level we maintained a stable EBT margin of 7.7% year-on-year. On a Group level, we generated more than € 10 billion in profit before tax. Without the burden of higher tariffs, both Group EBT and Automotive EBIT would have exceeded the previous year’s levels.

A decisive success factor in 2025 was our consistent cost discipline. As planned, we significantly reduced research and development expenditure and investments from their respective peak levels of 2024 and drove additional cost savings – without compromising our long-term competitiveness.

Early strategic investments, operational excellence and a strong financial foundation leave the BMW Group well positioned for the years ahead. As we launch the NEUE KLASSE product offensive and roll out its technologies across the entire portfolio, we will continue to create sustainable value for all stakeholders. I wish you an inspiring and informative read of the BMW Group Online Report.

Yours sincerely, Walter Mertl

BMW Group scores circular economy win with high-voltage batteries
As e-mobility grows, the BMW Group is focusing on circular economy: since November 2024, a Europe-wide partnership with SK tes has been recovering cobalt, nickel and lithium from used high-voltage batteries for reuse in new ones. The closed loop will expand to North America in 2026 – a key step towards resource efficiency and supply chain resilience.

Munich | Mobility, Ressources
BMW Group and E.ON introduce Germany’s first customer solution enabling electric cars to actively participate in the energy market
The BMW Group and E.ON are launching Germany’s first commercial vehicle-to-grid (V2G) solution for private customers. Thanks to bidirectional charging, starting with the BMW iX3* electric vehicles become an active participant in the energy market – able not only to consume electricity but also to feed energy back into the grid when needed. For the first time in Germany, electric vehicles can be used as flexible energy storage units in everyday life. Customers who make their vehicle battery available for smart charging and discharging can earn an annual bonus of up to €720.

Up and running: BMW Group and Encory launch innovative direct recycling to recover battery raw materials
The BMW Group has opened the Battery Cell Recycling Competence Centre in Salching, advancing circular economy goals. Its innovative direct recycling process mechanically dismantles battery cells and production residues, returning raw materials directly to cell manufacturing without energy-intensive chemical treatment.
