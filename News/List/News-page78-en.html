First Digital Positron Emission Tomography Scanner Unveiled, Enabling Earlier and More Accurate Cancer Diagnosis

The reporter learned from Wuhan Optoelectronics National Laboratory (Preparation) on December 10 that Xie Qingguo, a professor at Huazhong University of Science and Technology, led a scientific research team to successfully develop the world's first digital positron emission tomography (PET)...
2012-12-11
Digital PET Laboratory
REPORT (By Zhou Qianjin, Reporter Lu Wei)On December 10th, it was reported from the Wuhan National Laboratory for Optoelectronics that Professor Xie Qingguo of Huazhong University of Science and Technology led a research team to successfully develop the world's first digital Positron Emission Tomography (PET) device. The tumor detected by this digital PET is only one-twentieth the size of the smallest tumor detectable by currently commercialized PET devices, which helps in earlier and more sensitive detection of tumors for cancer diagnosis.
Professor Xie Qingguo introduced that the first digital PET has completed research on 13 mice models with lung cancer, liver cancer, ovarian cancer, etc., 16 mice models with Alzheimer's disease, and 30 normal mouse models. These studies have comprehensively validated the performance of the instrument, particularly confirming a significant breakthrough in spatial resolution.
Since 2001, Professor Xie Qingguo has led an interdisciplinary team involving medicine, engineering, science, among other fields (totaling 13 disciplines), to invent a 'multi-voltage threshold sampling method', successfully acquiring sufficient information for accurate measurement of the signal and achieving precise image reconstruction. Through collaborative innovation in learning, research, and production, they have completed the entire R&D process from theoretical discovery of digital PET, to industrial-scale production of key detectors, to assembly and animal imaging trials of commercial machines.
Professor Zhang Yongxue, President of the Nuclear Medicine Branch of China Nuclear Society and Professor at Huazhong University of Science and Technology Union Hospital's PET Center, stated that any improvement in resolution represents a revolutionary breakthrough in medicine, meaning extended life for patients and optimal treatment timing and precision for doctors; digital PET can elevate the performance of PET systems to a new level, enabling earlier detection and more accurate diagnosis of diseases.
Dr. Gao Jianmin, Professor at the University of Chicago and renowned expert in PET imaging, believes that Xie Qingguo has pioneered digital PET, with the most urgent task being the conversion of technology into industrial advantages, achieving significant upgrades and leapfrog development for China's cutting-edge medical imaging equipment.
