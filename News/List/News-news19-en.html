More Than 100 Patients Have Tried This 'Magic Helmet' at Tongji Hospital

Tongji Hospital and the Digital PET Laboratory jointly developed a helmet-style all-digital PET system for real-time dynamic brain imaging in different body positions.
April 2, 2025
Hubei Daily App

Tongji Hospital, affiliated with Tongji Medical College of Huazhong University of Science and Technology, and HUST's Digital PET Laboratory have jointly developed a helmet-style all-digital PET system. Patients can undergo real-time dynamic brain imaging in different positions and can even use a mobile phone during the scan. More than 100 patients have tried the system since clinical testing began at Tongji Hospital.

Conventional head CT and MRI primarily show anatomical structure, while PET can reveal molecular metabolism, receptor activity, and neurotransmitter processes in living subjects. Traditional PET systems are often combined with CT or MRI and are large and difficult to move; the helmet project was designed as a mobile, dedicated brain PET system.

After several years of development, the system can operate without an accompanying CT or MRI scanner and is less constrained by location or patient posture. Its hemispherical design reduces the number of detection channels, improves portability, and supports high sensitivity and spatial resolution approaching physical limits.

During rehabilitation, the system could observe metabolic changes in different brain regions while a patient moves, supporting individualized treatment plans. In neurosurgery, it could also help clinicians monitor lesions and improve the precision of surgical removal.

The team plans to integrate real-time helmet PET imaging with brain-computer interfaces, synchronize imaging and bioelectrical signal acquisition, and use artificial intelligence to fuse the data and map brain networks.
