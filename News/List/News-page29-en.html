Since the beginning of this year, in order to thoroughly implement the package of policies supported by the central government in Hubei and promote the early implementation, early effect and early benefit of the policies, the provincial party committee and provincial government have formulated a list of 108 key work responsibilities...
2021-06-23
Digital PET Laboratory

This year, to fully implement the central government's comprehensive support policies for Hubei and ensure early implementation, effectiveness, and benefits, the provincial Party committee and government have formulated a list of responsibilities for 108 key tasks. All regions and departments across the province are fully coordinating and implementing these measures with great effort, resulting in significant achievements in the implementation of central government's comprehensive support policies for Hubei, playing a crucial role in promoting post-epidemic recovery and high-quality development in Hubei.

High-tech innovation propels Hubei's takeoff

Supporting Hubei's epidemic control and key core technology breakthroughs. In 2020, the Ministry of Science and Technology launched a special activity called 'Science and Technology to Boost Economy 2020' in Hubei, allocating 265 million yuan to support 219 innovative projects in Hubei. A medical imaging tech company is collaborating with the Optics Valley Laboratory's research team on developing the second-generation digital PET equipment, which is one of these projects.

“This will significantly advance our ability to detect cancer earlier and also enable early diagnosis for brain diseases such as Alzheimer’s,” said Zhang Bo, Technical Director of Hubei Rui Shi Digital Medical Imaging Technology Co., Ltd.

Support funds boost Hubei's development

In addition to technological support, Hubei has made multiple efforts to secure a large amount of central government support funds. The Provincial Development and Reform Commission successively established financing cooperation mechanisms with nine banks, advancing project financing in batches and categories, reaching financing intentions for 5,525 projects, adding new credit lines for 1,447 projects, increasing the total credit line by 165.5 billion yuan, and disbursing 124.1 billion yuan by the end of 2020; The Provincial Department of Industry and Information Technology has also made efforts through 'striving, investing, lending, reducing', securing various funds totaling 92.325 billion yuan, successively establishing 'financial service oxygen chambers' with the Construction Bank, China Bank, and Industrial and Commercial Bank of China Hubei Branches to provide over 110 billion yuan in additional loans for small and medium-sized enterprises throughout the year.

“Securing a share of 10 billion yuan for national strategic investment at the same timeAlso securing board seatsThe first direct investment project of the National Fund has been established in Hubei.“This is what we have achieved,” said Leng Chengqiu, Deputy Surveyor of the Planning and Technological Transformation Department of the Provincial Department of Industry and Information Technology.

Major projects enhance public welfare

Utilizing central government's comprehensive support policies, Hubei has focused on key areas to plan a series of major projects aimed at addressing weaknesses, adjusting structures, and ensuring long-term benefits. In 2020, Hubei planned 2,247 public health system improvement projects with an estimated total investment of 254.5 billion yuan, focusing on the construction of 'four systems and one platform'. Currently, the Jingzhou Public Health Center project is under urgent construction; this is a dual-use comprehensive hospital for both regular medical services and epidemic control, having completed 85% of its total work.

“After completion, it will effectively be able to respond to any sudden public health emergencies in JingzhouMaximizing the protection of people's lives and ensuring patient treatment."Li Jun, the director of Jingzhou City Health Commission Project Office, said.

"By the end of 2020, a total of 1,037 projects had been initiated and constructed, with 261 completed. Currently, all secondary-level and above comprehensive hospitals in the province have established standardized fever clinics, reserved beds for infectious disease treatment and ICUs, and each county (city) has at least two nucleic acid testing institutions, effectively meeting current epidemic prevention needs," said Chen Fanjun, deputy director of the Planning and Information Department of Hubei Provincial Health Commission.

To stabilize enterprises and ensure employment, the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security and the State Council Poverty Alleviation Office launched a '6+1' labor cooperation special action. Hubei actively connected with seven eastern provinces and cities to sign labor cooperation agreements. A total of 67 dedicated trains and 25,598 dedicated buses were organized, directly transporting 705,000 migrant workers for employment outside their home areas, driving the cross-regional return-to-work employment of 71.7 million people who returned home before the Spring Festival.

“A total of 50.3 billion yuan in social security fees was waived throughout the year.Stabilization subsidies totaling 3.4 billion yuan were issued.Benefiting a total of 162,900 enterprises.Issued 11.6 billion yuan in entrepreneurial guarantee loans.First breaking through the one-hundred-billion-yuan mark.Achieved an additional urban employment of 751,800 people.Ensuring overall stability of the job market."Dong Changqi, a member of the Party Leadership Group and Deputy Director of Hubei Provincial Department of Human Resources and Social Security, said.

Last year, a large number of central enterprise executives also visited Hubei in quick succession to discuss cooperation. They signed 172 new project cooperation agreements with Hubei Province, with a total investment value of 463.61 billion yuan, achieving an initiation rate of 55.81%. To date, among the six aspects and thirty-one items of central support policies for Hubei, twenty-eight have been fully implemented, while three require continued implementation across years; according to our province's list of key work responsibilities, eighty-two items have been completed, with twenty-six still in progress, including sixty-nine planned key tasks for 2020 which were all accomplished as scheduled.

(Hubei Radio and Television Media Reporter Yang Lan, Xu Wei Yi, Feng Zhi Qiang; Editor-in-Chief Xiao Wen)
