The first Italian PhD student enters the Digital PET Overseas Research Centre

From the digital PET team of Huazhong University of Science and Technology, the Digital PET Morise Research Center established in Italy officially welcomed its first doctoral student this month - Emanuele, a 27-year-old Italian, who will study for a doctorate at Huazhong University of Science and Technology in September this year...
2018-01-22
Digital PET Laboratory

News has come from the Digital PET team at Huazhong University of Science and Technology that the Digital PET Molise Research Center, established in Italy, officially welcomed its first PhD student—27-year-old Italian Emanuele—in this month. He will enroll at Huazhong University of Science and Technology to pursue his doctorate degree starting from September this year. The arrival of the first PhD student marks the official launch of China's first overseas research center set up in Italy. Currently, the center has begun recruiting professors and students globally.

In July 2017, the Digital PET team reached an agreement with Molise Region in Italy to establish the Digital PET Molise Research Center together for brain science research. The Mediterranean Neurological Institute (NEUROMED) located in Molise, Italy, is one of the world's top brain science research centers and boasts Europe’s largest hospital specializing in neurological diseases, rich clinical experience in this field. Professor Xie Qingguo from Wuhan National Laboratory for Optoelectronics at Huazhong University of Science and Technology invented Digital PET technology and led his team to develop the world's first preclinical full-digital PET and clinical full-digital PET. The combination of China’s performance advantage in imaging equipment with Italy’s rich clinical experience is expected to form an international digital PET application high ground in brain science.

The launch of the Digital PET Molise Research Center has received significant attention from the Molise government. Recently, a grand press conference was held by the local government to warmly welcome the first Italian PhD student joining the center. Vincenzo Cotugno, President of Molise Region, said that the Digital PET Molise Research Center is now an important bridge for scientific and cultural exchanges between China and Italy. The development of Italy needs high-level talent teams like the Digital PET team. “We also hope Emanuele can achieve results in biomedical research and attract more talents to build their hometown with his exemplary role,” he added.

Italian Master's Degree Holder Chooses to Study for a PhD in China, Valuing the Leading Position of Digital PET Technology

Emanuele graduated from Rome University, one of Europe’s top universities, with a bachelor's degree in clinical medical engineering in 2013 and obtained his master's degree in biomedical engineering at Politecnico di Milano, Italy's number one engineering school, in 2016. He worked for half a year at the Digital PET Laboratory in Singapore where he achieved remarkable results. Last June, he completed research on tumor treatment with researchers from the Digital PET Laboratory, and the related findings were published in The Journal of Clinical Investigation, an internationally renowned medical journal.

Emanuele will conduct three years of research at the Digital PET Molise Research Center focusing on complex engineering problems (software and hardware). He will also enroll for his PhD degree at Huazhong University of Science and Technology starting from September this year. He said that he is a die-hard biomedical engineer who regards scientific research as the most noble challenge and fascinating career. Joining China's Digital PET team was partly because it is the birthplace of digital PET, with world-class research achievements and teams in the field.

On the other hand, Emanuele also values the huge application potential of PET in brain science. “Italy is one of Europe’s most aging countries; diseases like Alzheimer's and Parkinson's are mostly 'age-related'. Currently, there are no cure methods for these diseases. If early diagnosis and intervention can be made, survival rates and quality of life could significantly improve. PET has ultra-high biochemical sensitivity and can display biological molecule metabolism, receptor activity, and neurotransmitter activities in living organisms, making it a powerful tool for brain science exploration and neurological disease research,” Emanuele said with great anticipation. “This work will benefit all humanity infinitely.”

Furthermore, Molise is Emanuele's hometown, and he also hopes to make achievements there and give back to his home.

Emanuele mentioned that this isn't the first time he has worked with Chinese people. His experience in Singapore already exposed him to China’s culture and work style. Their diligence and intelligence left a deep impression on him. Emanuele believes that in recent years, China's development in biomedical engineering research has been particularly rapid, closely related to China's science and technology development strategy, the strong academic atmosphere of Chinese universities, and superior hardware conditions. “The Digital PET team is an excellent representative. The vibrant academic atmosphere, competitive scholarships, excellent living and working conditions all allow me to focus on my research and achieve my academic dreams,” he said.

Digital PET Overseas Research Center Gains Popularity

Nicola D'Ascenzo, a professor in the Digital PET team who is also from Molise, joined the team full-time in 2014. It was under his initiative that the establishment of the Digital PET Molise Center was facilitated. Nicola said that upon announcement of the center's establishment, it received numerous applications locally. Within just one month, fifteen Italian PhD students applied for positions at the center. In mid-February, Paolo, an Italian master’s student from the Digital PET team, and Li Jing, a Chinese PhD student, will also join Emanuele to work at the Molise Research Center. Nicola introduced that the preparatory work of the research center is currently proceeding in full swing, with support from the Molise government, procurement of scientific instruments has been completed. The center plans to gradually complete three stages of work: application of Digital PET helmets in brain tumor research, installation of preclinical full-digital PET equipment, and PET/MEG image fusion technology for early diagnosis of Alzheimer's disease.

Xie Qingguo said that previously, Chinese students mainly came from third-world countries. However, now more and more students from Western developed countries are choosing to come to China. This fully demonstrates that as long as it is an international cutting-edge technological platform, it has the attraction to gather talents, and China already possesses such appeal. “We need to further strive to convert our technical advantages into application advantages, truly transforming our technical confidence into broader social and cultural confidence. This is also the goal of our Digital PET team,” (China Daily Hubei Bureau).
