Remember to write a new chapter | Set sail under the guidance of the new development concept "14th Five-Year Plan"

Today, deputies to the National People's Congress participated in the two sessions...
2021-06-23
Digital PET Laboratory

Today, representatives and committee members who attended the National People's Congress (NPC) and Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference (CPPCC) returned to their workplaces in various forms to convey the spirit of the two sessions. Everyone expressed that President Xi Jinping has made a series of important statements regarding standing at a new stage of development, implementing new development concepts, building a new pattern for development, promoting high-quality development, and creating a high-quality life, which has rallied hundreds of millions of people with an unstoppable drive forward. The entire province is full of confidence to continue working in the posture of strivers as they embark on a new journey towards the 14th Five-Year Plan.

Early this morning, Yan Dapeng returned from Beijing to Wuhan and went straight to his workplace at Wuhan Raycus Fiber Laser Technology Co., Ltd. He started collaborating with his technical team to develop upgrades for fiber laser devices, aiming to increase their power output from 30 kilowatts to 100 kilowatts to meet customized laser welding needs, ensuring that more Hubei enterprises can access advanced manufacturing equipment. In Yan Dapeng's view, President Xi Jinping repeatedly mentioned high-quality development at the two sessions, which will be the main tone for China's future economic development.

National People's Congress Representative and Deputy Chairman & Chief Engineer of Wuhan Raycus Fiber Laser Technology Co., Ltd., Yan Dapeng: 'This morning we held a meeting to implement the important speech by President Xi Jinping. Our company is now taking action, focusing on innovation-driven independent research and development, increasing investment in R&D to reach 8% of sales revenue. We are actively recruiting high-level talents.'

Innovation factors are bursting with passion everywhere in Hubei. In the newly established Optics Valley Laboratory, technicians from the full-digital PET laboratory are working on system development for their second-generation PET medical equipment. Following President Xi Jinping's instructions to closely link innovation chains with industrial and supply chains, each manufacturing stage and component of their second-generation PET devices is an innovative topic.

Project Leader of Hubei Digital PET Engineering Laboratory Zhang Bo: 'The world's first full-digital PET mainly helped us solve the 0-to-1 process. We are now working on Full-Digital PET 2.0, a brand new technical route that revolves around the entire high-end medical device industry chain, conducting key technology research and breakthroughs at various innovation points. It is expected to be launched in the market by the end of this year.'

Putting ecological protection first demonstrates political awareness of green development. Remembering President Xi Jinping's instructions on ecological conservation, National People's Congress Representative Hu Wuqing returned to his village and led villagers to plant trees as their first task after returning from the sessions. He set a goal to expand the village’s 200-acre green industrial base to 500 acres, not only improving the living environment but also providing nearby employment opportunities for villagers.

National People's Congress Representative and Secretary of the Newgang Village Party Committee in Xiaochang County, Xian City, Hu Wuqing: 'In promoting rural revitalization, we must prioritize ecological protection. We firmly believe that green mountains and clear waters are as valuable as gold and silver mines. By leveraging our unique characteristics and advantages, we aim to strengthen the nursery and flower industry and expand high-quality fruit planting areas, enabling villagers to earn more income with hope.'

Hubei has never stopped strengthening ecological governance. In Wuhan, the Optics Valley Ecological Corridor project is in its final phase of tree planting and greening work, scheduled to open officially this May, forming a 10-kilometer-long green corridor connecting Jufeng Mountain and Longquan Mountain ecological zones.

Party Secretary and Chairman of China Yangtze River Eco-Environmental Protection Group Co., Ltd. Zhao Feng: 'The Optics Valley Ecological Corridor project itself is an important measure to implement President Xi Jinping's thoughts on ecological civilization, which integrates the construction of ecological civilization into the overall strategic layout of five-in-one, building a natural green ecosystem for urban water systems.'

Studying Party history education is also an essential part of this year’s spirit from the two sessions. This year, Hubei will carry out extensive Party history education across the province through micro-Party lessons, distinctive Party cultural education, and activities to make full use of its rich red resources to vividly tell stories about the Communist Party of China.

National Committee Member of the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference (CPPCC) and President of Hubei Socialist College Hu Zhongjun: 'We will organize experts to study Party history together, launch high-quality courses, use the wisdom of the Party to enhance our work performance.'

How can we effectively conduct Party history education? It involves integrating work with learning. Leveraging educational advantages, Wuhan University and some local enterprises have established demonstration points for Party building cooperation, holding monthly training sessions on 'Party history education', 'New China's history', etc., drawing continuous fighting strength from the study of Party history.

Party Committee Deputy Secretary of China Construction Second Engineering Bureau Hubei Branch He Liang: 'Visiting historically significant places in our country’s Communist Party history and organizing knowledge contests can attract more young people to participate, enhancing their understanding of Party history.'

(Reported by Feng Zhiqiang, Xu Wei Yi, Wang Yafeng; Edited by He Qianbin)
