I. Pain Points

Positron Emission Tomography (PET, Positron Emission Tomography) is one of the most advanced medical molecular imaging devices currently available, capable of non-invasive, quantitative, and dynamic assessment of living body functions, offering unique advantages in research and treatment for major diseases related to tumors, neurology, and cardiovascular conditions.

However, due to limitations imposed by ultra-high-speed scintillation pulse signal sampling issues, the technical principles of traditional PET have not seen breakthroughs over the past 40 years. Long-term challenges include 'inaccurate measurement, difficult use, and narrow application', which have constrained the full potential of PET from being realized.

II. Achievement Introduction

In response to the above issues, Professor Qingguo Xie from Huazhong University of Science and Technology led his team to propose the concept of 'Fully Digital PET' for the first time internationally in 2004. The team innovatively used 'Multi-Voltage Threshold Sampling' (MVT) to achieve precise digitalization of ultra-high-speed scintillation pulses, establishing a systematic and complete digital PET technology system through over two decades of accumulation. This pioneering work has opened up an important new direction in the international field of fully digital PET.

Digital PET technology is characterized by 'full digitization' and 'precise sampling': traditional PET must incorporate analog preprocessing circuits to indirectly obtain pulse information, which is inefficient; whereas fully digital PET achieves full digital signal processing while eliminating analog circuits, thereby achieving precise digitalization of signals at their source. This results in high-precision signal restoration with greater efficiency. These two characteristics bring revolutionary advantages such as 'superior performance, user convenience, and rapid manufacturing'.

The team has successively completed the world's first animal fully digital PET, the first clinical fully digital PET, the first standard CMOS silicon photomultiplier tube, and the first brain-specific fully digital PET. They hold complete independent intellectual property rights.

Focusing on key materials, core components, digital PET detectors, imaging methods, and applications in tumor diagnosis and treatment as well as brain science and diseases, the team has made scientific discoveries and technological inventions along the entire innovation chain from critical materials to system integration and application research. They have established a complete intellectual property layout covering eight countries and regions, ranking first globally in terms of patent numbers in the field of fully digital PET.

The 'Fully Digital PET Standards and Specifications Project', marked by multi-voltage threshold sampling technology, will be officially implemented in 2023, indicating that digital PET technology has taken the lead in this direction. This project fills a gap in industry standards for fully digital PET, clarifying its technological development and market orientation and further strengthening China's influence in high-end medical equipment innovation.

IV. Credentials and Honors

The team's related work has won the First Prize of Hubei Province Technical Invention Award (2013), three Gold Awards at the Geneva International Exhibition of Inventions (2013, 2017), the First Prize for Technological Invention in the Huang Jiashi Biomedical Engineering Award (2019). It was selected as one of the 'Top Ten Scientific and Technological Advances in China' by the Chinese Academy of Sciences and the Chinese Academy of Engineering in 2019, and among the 'Major Medical Progresses in China' for 2019. The team also led the formulation of digital PET technology standards and specifications in both China and Europe.

Five: Application Scenarios

The first generation of clinical full digital PET (model DPET100) received national innovative medical device special approval in 2018, market access in 2019, and was installed at Ezhou Central Hospital in 2020. It has been operating stably since then, serving over a thousand patients.

The second generation of equipment (model DigitMI930) obtained market access in 2022, achieving imaging with a scan time of 20 seconds per bed, making it the fastest imaging speed globally at present. This device has reached sales intentions with multiple hospitals. Through continuous iteration and optimization, full digital PET technology serves the public and maintains its advanced status.

Six: Market Prospects

The market is currently partially applied, with a potential scale of over 10 billion yuan.

1. Medical Imaging Aspect

The team has successively developed the world's first animal full digital PET and clinical full digital PET, achieving iterative updates to related equipment, bringing PET from an analog-digital hybrid era into a high-definition, precise quantitative digital era, capable of faster and more sensitive detection of tumors and other disease lesions. The team’s early work completed the research and development of 'better than others' DigitalPET 1.0, proving the feasibility and advantages of innovative technology routes. Future work plans to carry out 'unique' DigitalPET 2.0 innovation application demonstrations and system research and development, including brain PET, helmet PET, proton PET, etc., achieving breakthroughs that traditional PET cannot.

2. Radiation Detection Aspect

Digital PET is also widely used in nuclear radiation detection, security checks, oil exploration logging instruments, plant studies, and other fields. In the field of oil logging, the team has cooperated with domestic oil exploration giants to develop logging equipment based on MVT methods, which have already been applied in Shanxi, South China Sea, etc.; in the field of security check CT, cooperative development of security check CT can achieve material identification, which is significant for detecting prohibited items such as highly toxic substances and anesthetics, and has already entered pilot use.

3. Exploration of Scientific Frontier Applications

Digital PET can also be used in distributed observation of cosmic events; the cloud platform constructed based on MVT methods is more open and flexible, capable of adapting to complex application scenarios such as large scientific facilities; the team focuses on the development of next-generation ultra-fine three-dimensional coding, high-density readout PET detectors, providing better infrastructure for algorithm developers.
