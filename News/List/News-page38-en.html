■ Correspondent Yang Ya


On July 31st, in the fifth China 'Internet+' College Students' Innovation and Entrepreneurship Competition Hubei Province Preliminary Round, our university's teacher-student collaborative team 'Global First-of-its-kind Fully Digital PET System and Industrialization' successfully advanced through rounds to achieve the highest score in the main track ranking match! Including the 'Digital PET' project, a total of 6 projects from our university have qualified for the national finals, tying for first place among all participating universities. In early October, these six projects will travel to Zhejiang University to compete in the national finals. On May 31st this year, the clinical fully digital PET/CT system developed by Professor Xie Qingguo and his doctoral student Zhang Bo was approved by CFDA for market entry and sales.



Devoting all of my youth to the Digital PET cause


"I met Professor Xie Qingguo in my first year of university in 2005, when I was only 19 years old. He was 33 (the same age as me today). It's been 14 years since then. We have moved from research to entrepreneurship, dedicating all our youth to Digital PET." During the preliminary round speech, when Zhang Bo mentioned this point, a warm applause erupted throughout the venue, which was the only spontaneous applause for any project that day.


Nineteen years ago, young scholar Xie Qingguo chose PET as his research direction. In 2004, he invented digital PET technology. The same year, Zhang Bo came to Huazhong University of Science and Technology to study undergraduate, and joined Professor Xie's research team in 2005. Over the seven years from undergraduate to master’s degree, Zhang Bo witnessed the arduous journey of Digital PET transforming from theoretical invention into technology and products.


"I remember when Teacher Xie first proposed the concept of digital PET, it was not well accepted by the industry at that time. However, he firmly believed in the huge potential of this new technology and persisted in cultivating this uncharted territory with determination. It is precisely because of this perseverance that Digital PET began to produce a series of results gradually. Eventually, international medical equipment giants also recognized the value and potential of digital PET; Siemens proposed digital methods in 2009, followed by Philips proposing its own digital technology in 2010. Nowadays, among PET manufacturers, it seems like spring breeze overnight, with 'fully digital' everywhere. This made me realize a truth: on the path to pursuing truth, as long as you see clearly and firmly believe, persistently pursue your goal, you will surely see the moon after the clouds part!"


The seven years of studying at Huazhong University also gradually transformed Zhang Bo into Professor Xie's favored disciple. After graduating with a master’s degree, Zhang Bo was hired by a company as a technical director due to his outstanding abilities. "At that time, I wanted to see the world outside, but after working for some time, I felt like something was missing."


In 2014, Professor Xie Qingguo, who was preparing for clinical PET industrialization, approached Zhang Bo: 'Do you want a job where you can see your future clearly from day one, or do you prefer to have a lifelong career filled with challenges and opportunities?' For Zhang Bo, Digital PET is hard technology that he has been striving for over the past decade, possessing significant technological potential and application value. "What I was missing before was a sense of belonging to a cause that I would dedicate my life to. Digital PET is truly a cause that can change the world and benefit humanity; isn't this exactly what I have been seeking as a lifelong career?" It was with such thoughts that Zhang Bo gave up his high-paying position and joined in without hesitation.


"Starting a business is always the most challenging path!" Reflecting on the past 14 years of following his mentor into Digital PET, Zhang Bo feels deeply moved. Initially, there was no space for them to work; they could only set up base in an abandoned laboratory corner at school. In a forty-square-meter room, ten or so people squeezed together, moving tables back and forth repeatedly when assembling the prototype machine. At their most difficult times, because they couldn't pay salaries, Zhang Bo even took out personal credit loans from banks to provide living expenses for his team members.


"All efforts have not been in vain! The earlier we make fully digital PET available, the sooner China's high-end medical equipment can turn around and save more lives." For Zhang Bo, dedicating all of his youth over 14 years is just a starting point. In the second half of his life, he will continue to strive for Digital PET to benefit human health.


Focusing on one thing for nineteen years: turning around China's high-end medical equipment


PET is currently one of the most expensive medical imaging methods. Unlike common CT and MRI anatomical images, PET can detect abnormalities in biochemical reactions before lesions form, enabling early detection of disease.


PET primarily targets three major killers of human health: tumors, cardiovascular diseases, and neurological disorders. However, this equipment has the lowest domestic production ratio among high-end medical devices; meanwhile, existing PETs also fail to meet clinical diagnostic needs due to performance limitations and difficult applications.


Zhang Bo introduced that over the past 19 years, the Digital PET team has been dedicated to solving the most critical digitalization issues in PET research. Professor Xie Qingguo's MVT digital method opened up a new technical route for PET, not only resolving major technological bottlenecks but also further enhancing PET performance.


"China is currently the country with the highest incidence and mortality rates of cancer globally; Chinese people need their own high-end medical equipment rather than relying on imported instruments. We have focused on one thing for nineteen years, and will continue to do so in the future: making Digital PET a breakthrough point for China's high-end medical equipment turning around!" Zhang Bo believes this is an important reason why the Digital PET project won first place in this preliminary round.



The 'Internet+' College Students' Innovation and Entrepreneurship Competition of China is an essential carrier and platform for deepening innovation and entrepreneurship education reform. Since its inception in 2015, over five million college students from more than one million project teams have participated in the previous four sessions, producing a large number of high-quality projects with high technological content, market potential, and social benefits, making it the largest and most influential college student innovation and entrepreneurship event in China.
