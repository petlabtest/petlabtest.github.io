Xi Jinping Inspects Scientific and Technological Innovation Achievements in Anhui

During an inspection in Anhui, Xi Jinping visited an exhibition of major scientific and technological achievements at Hefei Binhu Science City and spoke with researchers and business representatives.
October 18, 2024
Xinhua

Xi Jinping conducted an inspection tour in Anhui from October 17 to 18, visiting historical and cultural areas as well as science and technology innovation parks in Anqing and Hefei.

At Hefei Binhu Science City, he viewed an exhibition of major scientific and technological achievements, learned about reforms to innovation mechanisms and efforts to accelerate technology transfer, and spoke with researchers and business representatives. Life and health technologies were among the high-tech fields represented at the exhibition.

Xi emphasized that science and technology should take the lead in advancing Chinese modernization and that innovation is an essential path forward. He called for faster progress toward high-level scientific and technological self-reliance and encouraged researchers to pursue bold innovation.

During a subsequent briefing by the Anhui provincial authorities, he also called for faster technological innovation and industrial transformation, stronger high-level research platforms, and closer integration of innovation, industry, capital, and talent chains.
