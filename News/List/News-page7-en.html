The world's first fully digital PET-CT, with multiple models of 'disease early warning machines' making their debut

As a representative achievement in China's high-end medical equipment sector—digital PET—the leading company Raysight Medical has brought four 'disease early warning machine' devices to this exhibition, all featuring entirely independent intellectual property rights.

Among them, the clinical whole-body fully digital PET/CT (DigitMI 930) boasts internationally leading comprehensive performance metrics. It can complete a full-body scan in just 135 seconds and handle up to 100 patients per day.

The amount of radioactive contrast agent used for the equipment check has also been reduced to one-third of that used in traditional PET, not only reducing radiation exposure during the examination but also being more friendly and safer for radiation-sensitive groups such as elderly people and children.

It significantly reduces hospital operating costs; at the same time, this product also offers higher image quality, with high-definition imaging helping doctors diagnose major diseases like cancer faster, more accurately, and earlier,earning it the description 'a game-changer'.

A true 'cancer early warning system'. Another brain-specific full-digital PET (DigitMI i30), which is currently the only commercial brain-specific PET product in China to have obtained a Class III medical device registration certificate.

The brain is the most intricate and complex organ in the human body, with extremely stringent requirements for equipment performance. Dai Jian introduced that compared to whole-body PET, DigitMI i30 offers superior spatial resolution, higher sensitivity, better temporal resolution, and improved image quality. It can achieve dynamic monitoring of brain functional activities.

It is particularly useful in diagnosing brain tumors, Alzheimer's disease, Parkinson's disease, epilepsy, and other neurological disorders.

It has great potential for early diagnosis and can be called a 'brain disease early warning system'.

In addition, Raycare Medical also showcased the company's conceptual product, reconfigurable PET, which is currently the only PET with variable scanning aperture. It can adjust its detection hole according to the size of the scanned object.

The world's first pulmonary gas magnetic resonance imaging system has completed product registration and been launched to the market.

Human pulmonary gas magnetic resonance imaging systems brought by Wuhan Institute of Polarized Medical Technology Co., Ltd. were developed by the Innovation Academy for Precision Measurement Science and Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS). The CAS also led United Imaging Healthcare in establishing Wuhan Institute of Polarized Medical Technology Co., Ltd. Currently, this technology has been approved for market release by the National Medical Products Administration of China. This is currently the world's first clinically approved multi-nuclear magnetic resonance imaging system capable of gas imaging, solving the clinical challenge of non-invasive and radiation-free precise detection of lung diseases.

It is understood that the ultra-sensitive MRI team at the Innovation Academy for Precision Measurement Science and Technology has beenworked intensivelyfor over a decade in areas such as hyperpolarization technology for enhancing gas magnetic resonance signals, ultra-fast pulmonary gas magnetic resonance imaging technology, and human multi-nuclear magnetic resonance imaging technology, achieving comprehensive breakthroughs.

The human pulmonary gas multi-nuclear MRI system developed by the team consists of two core devices: the 'Medical Xenon Gas Generator' (model: verImagin VIP510) and the 'Human Multi-Nuclear Magnetic Resonance Imaging System' (model: uMR 780(Xe)). These effectively address the challenge of extremely weak MRI signals caused by low gas density in lung detection, enabling a transition from single-nucleus to multi-nucleus MRI systems clinically. This has transformed lung cavity imaging diagnosis from 'unobservable' to 'clearly visible'.

This research was continuously supported by the National Natural Science Foundation of China's Major Research Instrumentation Program (recommended by government departments), CAS's Scientific Instrument and Equipment Development Project, and Key Special Projects for Technology Transfer and Transformation (Hongguang Special Project). In September 2020, the core equipment 'Medical Xenon Gas Generator' obtained the world's first registration certificate for similar medical devices; on August 16, 2023, the multi-nuclear MRI system was approved for market release, becoming the world's first clinically available multi-nuclear MRI product capable of gas imaging. Currently, this system has been applied in clinical research at over ten top-tier hospitals and scientific research institutions in Beijing, Shanghai, Wuhan, among others, with significant clinical implications for early detection, diagnosis, and treatment of lung cancer, chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD), and other lung diseases.
