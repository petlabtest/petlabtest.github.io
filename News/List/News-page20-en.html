This "Hubei Made" is included in the national digital diagnostic equipment industry technical standards

Recently, the "All-Digital PET Technical Standards and Specifications" led by a team of Chinese scientists formally submitted the comprehensive performance evaluation data of the project, and it is expected that the comprehensive performance evaluation of the project will be completed by the middle of this year...
2022-04-18
Digital PET Laboratory

Recently, the 'Full Digital PET Technology Standards and Specifications' project led by a team of Chinese scientists officially submitted comprehensive performance evaluation materials. It is expected to complete the comprehensive performance assessment in mid-year this year. This project drafted the 'Technical Requirements for Digitalized Positron Emission Tomography (PET) Devices', which was completed and approved in December 2021. This means that digital PET technology, which has already led the technical direction, officially received support from national standards and entered a new stage of development.

Xie Qingguo, inventor of digital PET and professor at Huazhong University of Science and Technology, believes that incorporating digital PET technology into industry standards not only fully demonstrates its leading position and maturity but also fills the gap in the evaluation system for full digital PET products. This is conducive to accelerating the integration of innovation chains and industrial chains in China's medical device sector, promoting China's digital diagnostic equipment as a whole to enter the forefront internationally.

In 2019, the Ministry of Science and Technology launched a special project on 'Full Digital PET Standards and Specifications' under the National Key Research and Development Program 'Digital Diagnostic Equipment'. The project was compiled according to the letter from the Medical Device Registration Department of the National Medical Products Administration regarding the allocation of project numbers for medical device industry standards in 2021. It covers five aspects: research on imaging performance based on full digital PET reconstruction methods, system performance testing and measurement method studies, system function and efficiency determination studies, non-clinical and clinical evaluation method studies, and standard research. This comprehensive initiative aims to update and formulate industry standards for PET, establishing a performance definition and evaluation system suitable for full digital PET products, thereby promoting the advancement of PET technology and market expansion.

PET stands for Positron Emission Tomography, which is a highly sensitive nuclear medicine molecular imaging technique. PET has significant application value in the early diagnosis, efficacy assessment, and pathological research of major diseases such as malignant tumors, neurological disorders, cardiovascular diseases, etc. Traditional PET technologies have been constrained by ultra-high-speed scintillation pulse signal sampling issues for 40 years, unable to break through their analog-digital hybrid technical route, with shortcomings including 'inaccurate measurement', 'difficult use', and 'limited application'. The team led by Xie Qingguo at Huazhong University of Science and Technology invented the MVT (Multi-Voltage Threshold) sampling technology in 2004. After 20 years of development, it has formed a full digital PET technology system characterized by 'full digitization' and 'precise sampling', pioneering and leading this new direction in medical imaging.

Xie Qingguo introduced that compared to PET technologies based on analog circuits, full digital PET possesses the following features: first, precise scintillation pulse sampling from the source significantly improves signal acquisition efficiency and accuracy; second, comprehensive digital signal processing with high anti-interference capability and stability, capable of algorithm-driven and data-driven operations; third, modularization and intelligence. These characteristics endow full digital PET with advantages such as stable performance, flexible structure, and accurate quantification, offering great potential and broad prospects in precision medicine and intelligent diagnosis.

Xiao Peng, a core member of the project team and professor at Huazhong University of Science and Technology, introduced that since May 2021, the globally first clinical full digital PET/CT developed by their team has been officially put into use at Ezhou Central Hospital in Hubei Province. It has operated stably, identifying cancer lesions for nearly a thousand patients ahead of time. The head of nuclear medicine at Ezhou Central Hospital, Jiao Cili, believes that the equipment could achieve uninterrupted operation for over a year and steadily increasing scanning cases since its first clinical use, fully demonstrating the feasibility and huge potential of this new technological route.

Zhang Bo, responsible for digital PET industrialization, stated that in the past, the lack of industry standards related to full digital PET led to disorderly product definitions and chaotic technical routes on the market, restricting the development and promotion of full digital PET technology. The 'Full Digital PET Standards and Specifications Project' established a solid technological foundation with multi-voltage threshold sampling as itssignaturedigitalization technology, completing the full digital PET standard by Huazhong University of Science and Technology's Digital PET Industrialization Unit (Hubei Rui Shi Company), China National Medical Products Administration Technical Review Center, Beijing Institute for Medical Device Control, and United Imaging. This standard will drive a series of changes in PET imaging methods, system architecture, clinical applications, promoting the development of high-performance products with specific functions, forming a new market.

Xie Qingguo believes that establishing technical standards and specifications in the digital PET field can break through the bottlenecks of traditional PET product definitions and evaluation methods, clarifying the direction for full digital PET technology development and market orientation. This not only helps to fully leverage the advantages and value of PET imaging technology, promoting healthy and orderly development of the PET market but also effectively utilizes 'standard dividends' to promote high-tech development while enhancing China's voice in the field of high-end medical device technological innovation. 'This also motivates our team to continuously convert technical potential into industrial momentum, leveraging innovative strengths, raising industry standards, and advancing China's high-end medical device industry towards achieving self-reliance and control strategic goals,' Xie Qingguo said (Economic Daily reporter Liu Jie).
