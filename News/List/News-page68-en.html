Innovation in Medical Device Technology in China
      "Detecting invisible rays, transforming them into PET images and radiation data; industrializing intangible knowledge and technology to truly serve society. 'Visualize the Invisible' (Xing qi wei jian), has been the Digital PET team’s mission for over a decade, supporting the PET laboratory and uniting innovative enterprises like Rayence. It is our direction in academic-industrial collaboration and also the core philosophy of the entire team," Professor Qingguo Xie from the Wuhan National Laboratory for Optoelectronics (WNLO) at Huazhong University of Science and Technology told reporters from China’s Ministry of Science and Technology. Recently, a project titled 'Development and Application of Ultra-High Resolution PET' led by Huazhong University of Science and Technology has been approved under the Major Scientific Instrument Development Program and is now entering its implementation phase. The industrialization task will be undertaken by Suzhou Rayence Technology Co., Ltd., ensuring that both large-scale clinical research PET prototypes and small-scale basic scientific research PET prototypes can quickly transition into marketable products. This marks a significant acceleration in the digital PET’s industrialization process, making it imminent for self-developed intellectual property to serve society through digital PET.
Transforming intangible rays into tangible form
Inside precise PET instruments lies an immensely complex system, yet its initial signal originates from a pair of gamma photons produced by the annihilation of positive and negative electrons. In the microscopic world, their speed is unparalleled and elusive, challenging countless scientists to capture them with all their ingenuity. The PET laboratory has charted an unconventional path. High-energy gamma rays, through a series of photoelectric conversions, generate high-speed electrical pulses that are not random but follow hidden patterns discovered after years of research at the lab. This led to the development of MVT (Multi-Voltage Threshold) methods based on waveform prior information. Professor Xie stated, “This is an innovative breakthrough in principle and methodology, revealing previously unseen natural laws and generating new knowledge, laying a solid foundation for subsequent technological innovations.”
"Visualize the Invisible" has dual meanings: one being PET technology’s ability to render invisible radiation visible through detectors, thereby converting internal bodily diseases into observable conditions. The deeper meaning lies in exploring unknown territories at the forefront of science, transforming them into societal-serving knowledge, technologies, and products that shape humanity's future. Based on MVT, new knowledge has been transformed into new technologies via innovative electronic system design and modular detector development; through research on novel image reconstruction methods and system integration, the world’s first fully digital PET—Trans-PET—was developed.
Discovering New Knowledge Through New Instruments
Industrialization is an inevitable path for Digital PET instruments. The enterprise partner Suzhou Rayence Technology Co., Ltd. has played a crucial role in this process. Professor Xie noted that enterprises, understanding market demands better, can guide instrument research towards practical applications, such as developing adaptive functions and variable-structure PETs, ensuring the developed instruments immediately maximize their utility in application fields; strict engineering standards from enterprises accelerate productization of research results, making lab prototypes stable like mature products ready to meet application unit requirements, quickly generating practical value. It is through this series of transformation processes that fully digital PET instruments not only visualize invisible rays and complete imaging but also, through industrial promotion, enhance their applicative value, leading to the discovery of new knowledge via new instruments, thus initiating a virtuous cycle.
Establishing an 'Academic-Industrial-Research-Use' Demonstration Center
This time, the PET laboratory’s acquisition of support under the National Major Instrument Program will seek greater technological breakthroughs on existing foundations. By developing second-generation digital PET detectors to achieve higher resolution and high-field compatibility; simultaneously advancing variable-structure imaging methods and data analysis techniques that match detector performance, ultra-high-resolution application-adaptive digital PET devices will be formed. The project will also integrate multimodal imaging instruments for comprehensive research while closely collaborating with application units to establish an 'Academic-Industrial-Research-Use' integrated PET application demonstration center. This initiative maximizes resource integration and its role in advancing the Digital PET industrialization process is self-evident, potentially forming a complete industry chain around PET.
Recently, under the leadership of Wuhan Optics Valley, a company relying on digital PET technology from the PET laboratory and focusing specifically on clinical PET medical imaging instruments is being actively prepared for establishment. It is expected to be officially registered within months, showcasing an essential link in the Digital PET industrial chain—clinical PET transitioning seamlessly from research to application.
