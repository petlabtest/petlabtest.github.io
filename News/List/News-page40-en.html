China's 'all-digital PET' crosses no man's land

version 01) Yang Ya, a correspondent for Liu Zhiwei, a reporter of this newspaper, recently announced the "Notice on the Release of Quasi-production Approval Documents" on the website of the State Drug Administration, and the "Positron Emission and X-ray Computerized Tomography Scanning System" (hereinafter referred to as "All-Digital PET") is on the list...
2019-06-20
Science and Technology Daily

(Science and Technology Daily June 20, 2019 Page 01) By Liu Zhiwei Correspondent Yang Ya

Recently, the National Medical Products Administration published an announcement of 'Approval Notice for Production Licenses' online. The 'Positron Emission Tomography and X-ray Computed Tomography Imaging System' (hereinafter referred to as full digital PET) is listed among them. This full digital PET medical device, which took 19 years of effort by Chinese scientists, represents a series of original technologies independently developed in China. Obtaining this license means that the domestically produced full digital PET has officially entered the market.

The relationship between full digital PET and traditional PET is akin to that between digital cameras and film cameras, marking a qualitative leap in technology with potential for comprehensive innovation in PET applications. Academician Jiazuan Ni of the Chinese Academy of Sciences believes that full digital PET, from key materials to core components and system integration, all developed independently by China, exemplifies representative achievements of independent innovation.

Made true international original

PET stands for Positron Emission Tomography (PET), a highly sensitive nuclear medicine molecular imaging technology. It has significant application value in the basic research, early diagnosis, and efficacy evaluation of major diseases such as malignant tumors, cardiovascular disease, and neurological disorders.

"In the past, PET products created by GPS (GE, Philips, Siemens) have made outstanding contributions to precise diagnosis and treatment of major diseases and established mature technical systems. Fully following GPS's technological route can significantly reduce product development risks but always leaves one in a follower position," said Qingguo Xie, the inventor of full digital PET and professor at Huazhong University of Science and Technology.

In 2001, Xie founded his laboratory. In 2004, he first proposed the MVT (Maximum Value Transformation) full digital sampling method; in 2009, he developed a fully digital modular PET detector. In 2010, the world's first digital PET scientific instrument was unveiled; in 2015, Ezhou Municipal Committee and Government listed digital PET as 'Project No. 1' for transformation, and that August, the world's first clinical full digital PET device was launched. In 2018, full digital PET/CT completed clinical trials.

Throughout this period, it was the Ministry of Science and Technology, National Natural Science Foundation of China, and various local science departments who recognized the enormous potential of digital PET and boldly acted as 'angel investors', supporting the team all along. Currently, Xie's team has cumulatively received over 200 million yuan in project funding and applied for more than 500 patents, with over 200 authorized.

"Now that digital PET has finally obtained its first medical device registration certificate, it means we have made true international original. This new technological route is proven to be viable and correct!" Xie said, "What makes us most proud is defining 'digital PET'. As a gold standard for measuring the advanced nature of PET products, digital PET has become a kind of standard and definition in some sense."

"In Hell Mode", Breaking Through Technical Routes

Zhang Bo, the person in charge of clinical full digital PET industrialization, revealed that under limited resources, this forensic product uses a system design with the highest technical challenge difficulty, akin to directly choosing 'Hell Mode' when playing games. By breaking through the most difficult mode, subsequent work becomes effortless.

"This fully demonstrates the enormous potential of digital PET as an entirely new technology," Zhang Bo excitedly said.

In fact, the advantages brought by PET digitization are not only reflected in its ability to detect smaller tumor lesions and achieve early diagnosis and treatment but also in its highly modular design that offers rapid research and development and assembly. According to Zhang Bo's introduction, it took only nine months from design to completion of the first clinical full digital PET prototype machine in 2015, with fewer personnel and resources than others.

Another example is the first brain-specific full digital PET installed at Sun Yat-sen University Affiliated First Hospital. This equipment was completed within three months and has already conducted multiple brain imaging trials, soon to be used extensively in diagnosing and researching major brain diseases such as Alzheimer's disease and Parkinson's disease.

According to data from national cancer registration points released in February 2017: the five-year survival rate for Chinese cancer patients is only 30.9%, less than half of that in the US. One important reason for low survival rates among Chinese cancer patients lies in delayed detection and treatment of cancer. It is known that currently, the number of PETs per million people in the US and Japan exceeds four, while China has fewer than 0.3 per million. Zhang Bo told reporters that once domestically produced clinical full digital PET achieves mass production, it can significantly lower the threshold for citizens to access PET examination services.

An Innovation Loop Is Formed, Hardly Vulnerable to Being Held Back

Xie Qingguo introduced that digital PET covers the entire innovation chain from principle, system to application. The industrial chain connects key materials such as scintillation crystals, core devices like SiPMs, critical components of digital PET detectors, animal full digital PET scientific instruments and clinical full digital PET medical equipment, up to imaging service applications, forming an innovation loop that is hardly vulnerable to being held back by anyone in terms of technology.

Weak light detection chips are the core devices of PET, long monopolized by the West. To avoid being constrained in the industrial chain in the future, Xie Qingguo's team started laying out research and development of new optoelectronic conversion devices from 2006 onwards, successfully developing standard CMOS process full digital silicon photomultipliers (SiPMs) in 2017 with performance reaching international advanced levels. Its successful development is significant for achieving independent innovation in China's weak light optoelectronic detection technology and enhancing the country's industrial core competitiveness.

"As the research high ground of digital PET forms, foreign top talents are being attracted to China, while we continuously export advanced technologies and products abroad," Xie Qingguo said. "With the enhancement of Chinese technological strength, the previous situation of talent outflow and technology inflow is reversing. Digital PET serves as a typical example."

"For a long time, the core technology of PET has been in the hands of international giants. Now that domestically produced full digital PET has obtained market access, the most critical thing is to quickly achieve mass production and grow strong, continuously improving the new technical route of full digital PET through large-scale application, so that high-end medical devices made in China can benefit the people as soon as possible," said Academician Ni Jiazuanexpressing great expectations for China's digital PET industry.
