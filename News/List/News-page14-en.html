2023-09-05
Science and Technology Daily

Science and Technology Daily reports (Reporter Wu Chunxin, Correspondent Yang Ya) On September 2nd, it was reported on the official website of the National Medical Products Administration that China's first brain-specific full digital PET (i.e., DigitMI i30) has been approved for registration as a Class III medical device and officially entered the market. This product is currently the only commercially available brain-specific PET in the world, marking new progress in clinical applications for brain diseases with innovative achievements in high-end medical devices.

As early as 2018, China's digital PET team completed the development of the principle prototype of DigitMI i30 and conducted a clinical study involving 374 cases at the First Affiliated Hospital of Sun Yat-sen University. The studies included early diagnosis of multiple neurological diseases such as Parkinson's disease, epilepsy, and Alzheimer's disease.

The team comprehensively refined the product from detector design to detection methods, imaging techniques, and diagnostic approaches, successfully developing the world's first brain-specific full digital PET this year, providing a critical tool for early diagnosis of degenerative brain diseases.

"Images of the human brain captured with digital PET show clear sulcal layers, distinct ventricular differentiation, and clear nuclear group resolution," said Zhang Xiangsong, head of the Nuclear Medicine Department at the First Affiliated Hospital of Sun Yat-sen University, who is responsible for the scientific clinical trial.

DigitMI i30 adopts an original MVT full digital sampling technology from the ground up. It further integrates unique technologies such as independent channel coupling, full data readout, zero light guide detection, and dynamic coincidence to achieve optimal spatial resolution of 1.2 millimeters across the entire field of view, uniform spatial resolution throughout the imaging range, and time-of-flight resolution of 249 picoseconds, among other ultra-high physical performance metrics.

In terms of application, DigitMI i30 achieves a current highest frame rate of 40FPS ('movie-level' high-frequency dynamic observation) to capture millisecond-scale brain metabolic changes and neuronal firing processes. It is expected to provide new tools for the study of mechanisms of brain diseases and behavioral consciousness mechanisms, leading to unprecedented discoveries in the exploration of physiological and pathological mechanisms.
