Xie Qingguo's Team "Digital PET" Research Progress Selected as China's Top Ten Science and Technology Progress News

the top ten scientific and technological progress news in China selected by academicians of both academies was announced, and "China's independent R&D of clinical all-digital PET/CT equipment was allowed to enter the market" was selected...
2021-01-25
Digital PET Laboratory

On January 11, the top ten scientific and technological achievements of China in 2019 selected by academicians from both the Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS) and the Chinese Academy of Engineering (CAE) were announced. 'Self-developed clinical full digital PET/CT equipment approved for market entry' was included among them. This achievement was completed by Professor Xie Qingguo's team from Huazhong University of Science and Technology.

It is understood that the clinical full digital PET/CT developed by Professor Xie Qingguo's team passed the registration approval of the National Medical Products Administration in late May 2019, obtaining market access and sales qualifications. This means that domestically produced full digital PET has broken through international technological monopolies, marking a significant breakthrough in China's high-end medical equipment development. PET stands for Positron Emission Tomography (PET), which is one of the most advanced molecular imaging technologies today. It holds great application value in the early diagnosis, efficacy evaluation, and pathological research of major diseases such as malignant tumors, neurological disorders, cardiovascular diseases, etc.

Professor Xie Qingguo's team invented the concept of 'digital PET' in 2004 and after more than a decade, established a complete technical system. The clinical full digital PET/CT equipment developed on this basis uses fully self-owned intellectual property rights technology with the essential characteristics of 'full digitalization' and 'precise sampling'. It fundamentally overturns the traditional PET technological route, enabling precise detection of the smallest lesion sizes and significantly advancing the time for cancer discovery. Currently, the team is dedicated to researching specialized new digital PET applications, including dynamic PET for awake and freely moving animal brain imaging, which will bring digital PET into a 'unique' 2.0 era.

From an original concept to developing a complete technical system, forming a system, becoming a product, entering the market, it took exactly nineteen years. It uniquely integrates three major yet mutually exclusive advantages of 'highly original', 'technically mature', and 'self-controlled'. This may bring about innovative development across the entire industry chain.

Recently, after completing certification for clinical full digital PET/CT, the first sale was completed, installed at Ezhou Central Hospital in Hubei Province, and will be officially put into clinical use after the New Year. In the future, the team will actively promote the application demonstration of digital PET, explore innovative systems and key applications of digital PET to enable more applications, better technical updates, and product iterations for digital PET, aiming to lower the threshold for ordinary people to access PET services as soon as possible, benefiting human health.

It is reported that China's top ten scientific and technological progress events are organized by CAS and CAE, hosted by the Department of Science and Technology of CAS and the Office of the Chinese Academy of Engineering, and co-organized by the China Science News. The annual selection activity has been held for 26 times to date. After extensive media coverage of the results, it has generated strong social reactions and played a positive and important role in promoting scientific and technological knowledge.

Previously, Professor Xie Qingguo's team was nominated for 'technological breakthroughs in PET digitalization' and 'successful development of clinical full digital PET in Wuhan' as progress items in 2012 and 2016 among the top ten scientific and technological news events in China.
