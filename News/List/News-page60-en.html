National Major Scientific Instrument Program Delivers Another Result: Digital PET Helps Identify a New Approach to Treating Fatty Liver Disease

2017-03-04

Xinhua News Agency Wuhan, March 4 (Yang Ya) Recently, the National Major Scientific Instrument Project 'Development and Application of Ultra-High Resolution PET' has achieved another significant result. The project 'Basic Biomedical Research Based on Ultra-High Resolution PET', jointly conducted by Wuhan University and Huazhong University of Science and Technology, recently published an important article titled 'Targeting CASP8 and FADD-like Apoptosis Regulator Ameliorates Nonalcoholic Steatohepatitis in Mice and Nonhuman Primates' (Targeting CFLAR Improves Non-Alcoholic Fatty Liver Disease in Mice and Non-Human Primates) in the international top journal Nature Medicine.

The article states that China has made new breakthroughs in treating fatty liver, with digital PET technology playing a key supporting role.

Digital PET aids in discovering critical molecules for inhibiting liver lesions

Recently, the National Major Scientific Instrument Project 'Development and Application of Ultra-High Resolution PET' achieved significant results. Professor Li Hongliang's team from Wuhan University used full-digital small animal PET to perform whole-body dynamic imaging on mice fed a high-fat diet, discovering that the natural immune molecule CFLAR plays a key inhibitory role in liver fat lesions, providing a living and dynamic quantitative observation method for deriving scientific theories about treating non-alcoholic fatty liver disease.

This research result was published recently by Professor Li Hongliang's team in the international top journal Nature Medicine. Non-Alcoholic Fatty Liver Disease is the most prevalent chronic liver disease in China, and this achievement may benefit over 150 million Chinese patients. The Nature Medicine journal believes that this study represents another world-class breakthrough made by Chinese scientists in the field of liver metabolic diseases, offering new insights into the pathogenesis of non-alcoholic fatty liver disease, laying a theoretical foundation for developing targeted therapeutic drugs, and having significant clinical guidance significance for preventing and treating this major chronic illness.

Digital PET technology has led to multiple major research achievements

In 2010, Professor Xie Qingguo from Huazhong University of Science and Technology led his team in developing the world's first small animal digital PET. With support from the National Major Scientific Instrument Project 'Development and Application of Ultra-High Resolution PET', development and application of digital PET instruments have advanced to a new level, leading to numerous scientific achievements across various fields.

Relying on digital PET technology, in lung cancer research, Professor Shi Huanzhong's team at Capital Medical University achieved significant results regarding the etiology of malignant pleural effusion, which is of great significance for guiding clinical diagnosis and treatment of malignant tumors; in liver cancer research, Academician Chen Xiaoping's team from Huazhong University of Science and Technology Tongji Hospital conducted studies on tumor metabolism to explore mechanisms of cancer cell metastasis, providing new ideas and methods for the clinical diagnosis and treatment of liver cancer; in depression research, Professor Li Weidong's team at Shanghai Jiao Tong University established a novel mouse model of depression, contributing to human efforts against depression; in vascular dementia research, Professor Chen Jianguo's team from Huazhong University of Science and Technology developed new drugs for alleviating vascular dementia with better therapeutic effects than traditional medications. These achievements have been published in authoritative journals and have attracted widespread social attention.

Currently, the Digital PET team has developed an innovative entity dedicated to providing scientific and technological services by leveraging the performance advantages of their instruments and drawing on accumulated experience from previous research collaborations. Dr. Wan Lu, responsible for digital PET application research, introduced that the team is committed to offering molecular imaging services in high-tech fields such as basic medical research and new drug development, hoping that Digital PET technology can establish a pioneering ground for scientific innovation and promote advanced medical imaging instruments' role in medical research.

Major scientific instruments have become essential means to secure strategic positions in science and technology.

Major scientific instruments have become essential means to secure strategic positions in science and technology. Original scientific instrument equipment often opens up new fields of study, brings about groundbreaking results, and expands human capabilities to understand and transform the world. However, for a long time, China's self-developed scientific instruments were very weak, especially high-end scientific instruments which heavily relied on imports, becoming a constraint factor in enhancing independent innovation capability and building an innovative country.

Practical evidence shows that material conditions for technological development are crucial supports for national economic growth and industrial competitiveness, as well as material guarantees for environmental protection, food safety, medical health, and other social and livelihood developments. The core major scientific instruments needed to foster new economic growth points and serve hotspots of public welfare cannot be solely relied upon imports; effective measures must be taken to vigorously promote the development of major scientific instruments. For this reason, in 2011, the central government's finance began implementing a National Major Instrument Program with total funding close to 13 billion yuan. To accomplish tasks well, one must first have good tools. The National Major Instrument Program has played a significant supporting role in helping scientists discover new data, verify new principles, and reveal new laws.

At the tenth China Scientific Instruments Development Annual Conference, Deputy Director Wu Xueti of the Ministry of Science and Technology's Department of Research Conditions and Financial Affairs once introduced that during the '12th Five-Year Plan' period, the Ministry of Science and Technology initiated 208 national major scientific instrument equipment development projects, achieving certain results in supporting national major needs and leading technological innovation.

For a long time, China's social capital investment has been at an early stage. In a sense, it is the Ministry of Science and Technology, National Natural Science Foundation Committee, and other science and technology administrative departments that have become visionary 'venture investors'. In fact, supporting scientific instrument development for independent innovation (independent innovation) is not something that can be achieved overnight. For example, Digital PET technology has undergone 16 years of research and development since 2001 to gradually produce a series of results; however, to enhance its application depth and breadth and truly establish the market, there is still a long way to go. Xie Qingguo frankly admits that compared with developed countries, we have advantages in technology but are at a disadvantage in terms of market, sales, brand, management, resources, etc. Currently, some research institutions in developed countries and industrial giants such as General Electric from the US, Siemens from Germany, and Philips from the Netherlands have achieved global monopolies in high-end medical equipment.

Despite numerous difficulties, Xie Qingguo still believes that scientific technology based on principle innovation will generate extremely high technological potential energy, which can be transformed into high industrial kinetic energy and maintain sustained driving force for industry development. Fully digital PET is a good product combining scientific discovery to technical invention and then driving industrial development, with the potential to bring leapfrog development to emerging strategic industries such as China's high-end medical equipment.
