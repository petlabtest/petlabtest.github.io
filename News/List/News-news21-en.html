The General Secretary Visits Us

Xi Jinping viewed an exhibition of major scientific and technological achievements at Hefei Binhu Science City and spoke with researchers and business representatives.
October 17, 2024
Anhui Daily Visual News Center

On October 17, Xi Jinping visited Hefei Binhu Science City, viewed an exhibition of major scientific and technological achievements, learned about local reforms to innovation mechanisms and efforts to accelerate technology transfer, and spoke with researchers and business representatives.

Raycan Technology executive Zhang Bo said the team would continue to promote the large-scale application of digital PET, speed the conversion of innovative technologies into products, and work to make advanced medical equipment more affordable and accessible.

The related footage was produced by the Anhui Daily Visual News Center, with filming and editing by Li Ji, planning by the center, and editing by Liu Ziling.
