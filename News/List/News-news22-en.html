A Global High-End 'Cancer Early-Warning System' Is Now Made in Hubei

The new domestically developed ApexMI all-digital PET/CT series debuted in Yichang, offering 1.3-mm image precision and a 60-second whole-body scan.
August 9, 2026
Hubei Daily

The new domestically developed ApexMI all-digital PET/CT series debuted at a high-end medical equipment event in Yichang on August 6. The system can achieve image precision of 1.3 mm and complete a whole-body scan in 60 seconds, filling a gap in local high-end PET-CT manufacturing.

Developed independently by Yichang Raycan Medical Technology, the ApexMI series incorporates multiple intelligent platforms for clinical and research use. The flagship ApexMI X35 uses a new-generation detector for higher sensitivity and applies a software-defined architecture to expand non-image disease-signal analysis and AI-enabled molecular imaging.

Experts described all-digital PET as an innovation rooted in fundamental principles and said the company's development reflects the broader transition of Chinese high-end medical equipment from following international leaders to competing alongside and, in some areas, leading them. The team has worked on all-digital PET for years and helped domestic high-end PET equipment progress from concept to product.

Raycan's Yichang facility has entered trial production and begun accepting orders. At full capacity, the production line is expected to manufacture 30 all-digital PET/CT systems a year while supporting an integrated chain spanning equipment development, clinical diagnosis, and data services.
