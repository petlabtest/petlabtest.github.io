Original Innovation in China! Professor from Huazhong University of Science and Technology's Research Breaks Monopoly, With Over 500 Patent Applications for One Invention

After 19 years of hard work, Chinese scientists have developed the world's first fully digital PET scanner with independent intellectual property rights. This breakthrough will break the long-standing monopoly of imported instruments in China’s PET market.
June 5, 2019
News Center of Huazhong University of Science and Technology

Recently, the National Medical Products Administration announced online that it has issued a production permit for the 'Positron Emission Tomography and X-ray Computed Tomography Imaging System' (referred to as fully digital PET). This is the result of 19 years of effort by Chinese scientists who have developed this fully digital PET with a series of independent original technologies. It means that China's fully digital PET can now enter mass production, breaking the long-standing monopoly of imported instruments in the domestic PET market. The inventor of this PET is Professor Xie Qingguo from Huazhong University of Science and Technology.

I. True International Originality Defining 'Digital PET'

PET, or Positron Emission Tomography, is an important medical device for precision medicine, mainly used in the diagnosis of tumors and cardiovascular diseases. It is one of the three major imaging devices alongside CT and MRI. For a long time, due to extremely high technical barriers, PET research has been monopolized by Western multinational giants, becoming a bottleneck in China's healthcare sector.

In 2001, Professor Xie Qingguo established his laboratory. In 2004, he first proposed the MVT full digital sampling method; in 2009, he developed the world's first fully digital and modular PET detector. In 2010, the world's first digital PET scientific instrument was born; in 2015, Ezhou Municipal Committee and Government listed Digital PET as 'Project No. 1' for transformation, with the global first clinical full digital PET device emerging that August. In 2018, the full digital PET/CT completed clinical trials.

  1. 2001: Team established;
  2. 2004: Proposed MVT full digital PET method;
  3. 2007: Achieved the world's first flat-panel PET;
  4. 2009: Developed fully digital and modular PET detector;
  5. 2010: The world's first digital PET scientific instrument was launched;
  6. December 2013: The first digital PET was launched;
  7. August 2015: The world's first clinical fully digital PET device was launched;
  8. November 2017: The world's first proton PET prototype machine was successfully developed;
  9. August 2018: Fully Digital PET/CT completed clinical trials;
  10. September 2018: The world's first brain-specific fully digital PET was successfully developed;
  11. June 2019: Fully Digital PET received approval from the National Medical Products Administration for production.

Throughout this period, it was the Ministry of Science and Technology, National Natural Science Foundation of China, and local science departments that recognized the huge potential of Digital PET, acting as angel investors to support the team's journey. Currently, Professor Xie Qingguo’s team has received over 200 million yuan in various project funds, with more than 500 patent applications and over 200 authorized patents.

"Now that Digital PET has obtained its first medical device registration certificate, it means we have truly achieved an international originality. This new technological route has been proven to be viable and correct!" Professor Xie Qingguo said, "What makes us most proud is defining 'Digital PET'. As a gold standard for measuring the advanced nature of PET products, Digital PET has become a benchmark in some sense."

II. Clearing the Path Under 'Hell Mode' Mass Production Will Benefit the Public

Zhang Bo, responsible for the clinical full digital PET industrialization, revealed that under limited resources, this certified product uses the most technically challenging system design, akin to choosing 'hell mode' directly in a game. By overcoming the hardest challenges, subsequent work becomes much easier!

In fact, the advantages of PET digitization are not only reflected in its ability to detect smaller tumor lesions for early diagnosis and treatment but also in its highly modular design which allows for rapid research and assembly. According to Zhang Bo, it took just nine months from design to production of the first clinical full digital PET prototype in 2015, with fewer personnel and resources than others.

In July 2018, the clinical full digital PET completed clinical trials at Sun Yat-sen University's First Affiliated Hospital and Cancer Hospital in Guangzhou. The trial verified its safety and effectiveness for imaging diagnosis. Mr. Luo, a 38-year-old nasopharyngeal cancer patient who participated in the trial, was found to have metastasis of cancer cells in his shoulder blade, rib, and femur using Digital PET. This early detection and precise localization enabled timely treatment, leading to his current recovery.

According to data from national cancer registration points released in February 2017: China's five-year survival rate for cancer patients is only 30.9%, less than half of that in the US. One major reason for low survival rates among Chinese cancer patients lies in delayed diagnosis and treatment. Currently, there are more than four PETs per million people in the US and Japan, while China has fewer than 0.3 per million. Zhang Bo told reporters that once mass production of domestically produced clinical full digital PET is achieved, it will significantly lower the threshold for public access to PET examination services.

III. Breaking Monopoly Forming an 'Innovation Loop'

Professor Xie Qingguo introduced that Digital PET covers the entire innovation chain from principle, system to application. The industrial chain connects key materials such as scintillation crystals, core devices like SiPMs, digital PET detector components, animal full digital PET scientific instruments, clinical full digital PET medical equipment, and imaging service applications, forming an innovation loop where no one can easily hold us back technologically.

Weak light detection chips are core devices in PET that have long been monopolized by the West. To avoid being constrained in the industrial chain in the future, Professor Xie's team started developing new optoelectronic conversion devices from 2006 and successfully developed standard CMOS process full digital silicon photomultiplier (SiPM) in 2017 with performance reaching international advanced levels. Its development is of great significance for independent innovation in China's weak-light optoelectronic detection technology and for enhancing the country's industrial core competitiveness.

With the formation of a Digital PET research high ground, foreign top talents are being attracted to China, while we continuously export advanced technologies and products overseas. As Professor Xie Qingguo said, "With the enhancement of China's technological strength, the situation where talent is exported and technology imported is reversing. Digital PET is a typical example."

Academician Ni Jiazuan expressed great expectations for China’s digital PET industry: “For a long time, the core technologies of PET have been in the hands of international giants. Now that domestically produced full digital PET has obtained market access, the key is to mass-produce and grow strong as soon as possible, continuously improving the new technological route through large-scale applications, so that high-end medical devices can benefit the public earlier.”
