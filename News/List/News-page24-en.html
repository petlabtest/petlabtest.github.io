The digital PET team won the 2021 "Hubei Youth May Fourth Medal"

In the 2021 "Hubei Youth May Fourth Medal" commendation list, the Digital PET Aid Medical Team of the School of Life Science and Technology of Huazhong University of Science and Technology was selected as one of the 15 winning groups...
2021-06-23
HUST College of Life Science and Technology

In the list of honorees for the 'Hubei Youth May Fourth Medal' in 2021, the Digital PET Medical Team from Huazhong University of Science and Technology's School of Life Sciences and Technology was selected as one of the fifteen award-winning groups. Upon learning about their recognition, team member Xiao Peng said, “This is a great affirmation and encouragement for our team members and teachers. For young China, we must sing its praises. Just like our laboratory’s slogan since its establishment: passion, courage, ideals, responsibility! We hope to cultivate young companions with vision, ability, and character, and work together to contribute to the country through science and technology!”

The Digital PET Medical Team is composed of members from Huazhong University of Science and Technology's School of Life Sciences and Technology led by Professor Xie Qingguo. At the beginning of 2020, when the novel coronavirus ravaged Hubei Province, the Digital PET Laboratory team quickly formed a 'Digital PET Aid Wuhan Medical Team' to contribute in various ways to the fight against the epidemic.

Professor Xiao Peng is an expert in biomedical engineering. He recalled that after the outbreak of the pandemic and with shortages of supplies, they temporarily formed an online committee of less than 20 people on WeChat groups for 'epidemic assistance'. Using his own resources, he collaborated with a friend at Tongji Hospital to collect and analyze local hospital needs, while other members raised funds and provided material aid.

Professor Xie Qingguo, the inventor of Digital PET and captain of the Aid Wuhan Medical Team, was stranded outside Hubei Province. He overcame difficulties to engage in rounds of discussions and collaborations with domestic and international medical teams, using digital PET technology to precisely depict the mechanism and progression of COVID-19. Simultaneously, he swiftly deployed support for the installation of PET/CT equipment at local hospitals.

Donated infusion pumps and disinfectants to hospitals.

Doctoral students Zhang Bo and Fang Lei from the Digital PET Laboratory directly rushed to the frontlines of the pandemic. With their actions of charging forward and facing danger head-on, they embodied the spirit of a new generation of Communist Party members. At that time, the Li Zihu Lake Branch Hospital of Hubei Integrated Traditional Chinese and Western Medicine Hospital was requisitioned as an epidemic control designated hospital and urgently sought CT equipment for case monitoring. The hospital had just completed its infrastructure but lacked complete facilities. Zhang Bo and Fang Lei installed, debugged, and tested a full-digital PET/CT in less than 48 hours, completing the delivery at 3 AM on January 25th. The temporarily formed 'Digital PET Aid Wuhan Medical Team' then provided round-the-clock support to the hospital, assisting doctors for 35 days.

It is known that the Digital PET team from Huazhong University of Science and Technology has been dedicated to researching digital PET technology for over twenty years, solving international challenges in high-speed pulse precise digitization. They have established a new technological system characterized by 'full-digital' and 'precise sampling', addressing critical issues in the field of advanced medical equipment technology. The global first clinical full-digital PET/CT medical device they developed was approved with a national medical device registration certificate in 2019, began trial operation at Ezhou Central Hospital in Hubei Province in June 2020, and has since identified cancer lesions for hundreds of patients early on.

The Digital PET Aid Wuhan Medical Team urgently supported hospitals in Ezhou.

In scientific research, the Digital PET team is committed to innovation and scaling new heights. They also place great emphasis on moral education. Professor Xie Qingguo believes that 'becoming a good person is more important than becoming talented; growing up is more important than achieving success. We absolutely do not welcome sophisticated self-interested individuals. The goal of talent cultivation in top universities should be to foster individuals with lofty ideals and broad-mindedness who are willing to undertake great endeavors.'

Adhering to such educational philosophies, the Digital PET Laboratory places particular importance on nurturing students' sense of social responsibility. In response to major events, both teachers and students actively participate. With nearly two decades of teaching experience, Professor Xiao Peng said, 'The core of the May Fourth spirit, which includes patriotism, progress, democracy, and science, is patriotism. As frontline educators, the May Fourth spirit provides us with profound practical enlightenment and rich spiritual nourishment in moral education.'

(Hubei Daily Full Media Reporter Fang Lin; Communicator Wang Xiaoxiao)
