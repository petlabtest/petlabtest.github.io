China's first! Wuhan's independent research and development of clinical all-digital PET sales overseas

Recently, Ruishi Medical, a digital PET industrialization unit located in Wuhan, signed a formal contract with the Fourth Hospital of Mongolia, realizing the first overseas sales of clinical all-digital PET medical equipment...
2023-09-05
Economic Daily

Recently, the digital PET industrialization unit Ruisi Medical in Wuhan signed a formal agreement with Mongolia's Fourth Hospital, marking the first overseas sale of clinical full-digital PET medical equipment.

Full Digital PET was proposed by Professor Qingguo Xie from Huazhong University of Science and Technology in 2004. He introduced the Multi-Voltage Threshold Sampling (MVT) method to solve the world's challenging problem of high-speed pulse digitalization for PET, achieving full self-research and development of Full Digital PET technology paths, key materials, core components, and system integration. This innovation also pioneered a new direction in medical imaging. In 2022, the team won the World Intellectual Property Organization Global Award based on their original MVT method.

Moreover, at the recent China-Mongolia Economic and Trade Cooperation Forum, Ruisi Medical, as the only Chinese enterprise representative in the medical device sector, reached a deep cooperation agreement with the Mongolian health delegation. They will upgrade and build the Central Asian Molecular Imaging Center based on the full-digital PET to be installed at Mongolia's Fourth Hospital, further promoting the application of Full Digital PET in Mongolia and the five Central Asian countries, contributing to local healthcare development.

The director of Mongolia's Fourth Hospital, Zorgagzar, stated that as Sino-Mongolian cooperation grows closer, Mongolia is seizing opportunities to enhance its domestic medical standards. As a clinical user, introducing China’s innovative technology into Mongolia holds significant importance for the health and well-being of Mongolians.

It is known that 80% of Mongolia's national population of three million resides in Ulaanbaatar. Zorgagzar introduced that applying high-performance PET equipment can not only address early diagnosis issues for major diseases but also leverage the precise quantitative data advantages of digital PET to rapidly collect large-scale disease data from the Mongolian population, understand regional disease patterns, and provide foundational data and decision-making references for Mongolia's health policies, benefiting the health of its people.

Zhang Bo, General Manager of Ruisi Medical, introduced that since 2017, animal full-digital PET scientific instruments have been installed in leading medical institutions and research institutes globally, including Finland’s National PET Center and Italy’s Mediterranean Neurological Institute. Transitioning from overseas research applications to clinical use, Digital PET as a representative of domestic original high-end medical equipment has achieved cooperation with Mongolian healthcare institutions and participated in high-level Sino-Mongolian meetings, fully demonstrating the company's international development potential and reflecting the positive trend of promoting domestically developed high-end medical devices to 'Belt and Road' countries.

Currently, clinical full-digital PET, from its first official installation in Ezhou, Hubei Province, has been steadily operating for three years. Its research continues to deepen, with the team developing the world's first standard CMOS silicon photomultiplier and an ASIC detector chip based on the MVT method, aiming to drive related industrial chains including rare earth scintillation crystal materials, new optoelectronic devices, intelligent software, etc., and will create a brand-new health industry market in the future.
