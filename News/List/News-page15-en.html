Acceleration of Domestic Fully Digital PET Industrialization and Application, Blossoming in Multiple Locations Including Beijing, Anhui, Shandong

By Liu Jie, Economic Daily News Client; Correspondent: Yang Ya

2023-04-09 11:59

China's independently developed high-end medical equipment, digital PET, has made new progress in clinical application. On April 8th, DigitalPET industrialization unit Ruisi Medical showcased at the 2023 World Big Health Expo and jointly held a signing ceremony for 'DigitalPET Innovation Synergy Strategic Development' with guests from all over the country. They also reached comprehensive cooperation agreements on clinical applications and scientific research exploration with medical institutions and research institutes in Anhui, Shandong, Beijing, and other regions.

In Ruisi Medical's 500 square meter exhibition hall, the team's independently developed next-generation fully digital PET/CT DigitMI 930 was unveiled. Last December, this equipment passed China’s medical device registration certification and officially entered the market. Relevant indicators show that DigitMI 930 can complete single-bed scanning imaging in just 20 seconds, with a full-body scan taking only 80 seconds, making it the fastest single-bed imaging speed globally.

Zhang Bo, responsible for industrialization of DigitalPET team and General Manager of Ruisi Medical, introduced that the world's first clinical fully digital PET, born in Ezhou, Hubei Province, was approved to enter the market in 2019, marking a breakthrough from research and application zero to one. Since then, the team has been dedicated to realizing large-scale, high-quality applications of DigitalPET to accelerate its development into a fast lane.

It is understood that at this signing ceremony, the team reached agreements on clinical application, research, and talent cultivation with Linyi City Traditional Chinese Medicine Hospital and Peking University Medical Department; on PET proton knife diagnosis and treatment integrated equipment development with China Academy of Ion Medical Technology Equipment Co., Ltd.; and on establishing a DigitalPET county-level application demonstration base with Feixian County People's Hospital in Shandong Province, known as one of the world’s longevity regions. Additionally, they reached clinical application cooperation agreements with Anhui Haici People's Hospital Company Limited and other units.

Ma Jie, Deputy County Magistrate of Feixian County Government, believes that Ruisi Medical establishing a DigitalPET county-level application demonstration base in Feixian County lays a solid foundation for precise diagnosis and effective treatment of cancer patients in the county. It provides more choices for medical care for the general public and solves the problem of having to go to large city hospitals for major diseases. This is a good deed that benefits the people. 'We hope that Feixian County People's Hospital can take this signing activity as an opportunity, fully leverage the advantages of PET-CT in precise diagnosis and treatment of malignant tumors and promoting key discipline development, contributing to high-quality development of health care.'

Lu Yanhui, associate researcher at Peking University Medical Department, introduced that Peking University Medical integrates teaching, research, and medical services, with a large number of internationally and domestically renowned experts in medical education, research, and clinical practice. Being able to collaborate with China's representative high-end medical equipment industry Ruisi Medical is full of anticipation. 'We hope through the cooperation between medicine and engineering, we can contribute to Healthy China together!'

Chen Fang, Deputy General Manager of Ruisi Medical, stated that in recent years, the team has focused on original innovation in high-end medical equipment, independent development of core technologies, and integrated research and development of whole machine systems. The related industry development responds to the national demand for 'accelerating remedying China's shortcomings in high-end medical equipment, achieving self-reliance and control over high-end medical equipment', representing the strategies of 'Innovative China' and 'Healthy China'.

Ruisi Medical's core technology fully digital PET technology originates from Professor Xie Qingguo, a recipient of the National Science Fund for Distinguished Young Scholars, who created the multi-voltage threshold sampling method (MVT). This technology solves the problem of precise digitization of high-speed pulses and has won China Patent Gold Award in 2021 and WIPO Global Award in 2022. The world's first clinical fully digital PET being approved to enter the market was selected as one of 'China’s Top Ten Scientific Advances in 2019' and 'Major Medical Progresses in China in 2019'. Currently, the team has applied for over 700 patents covering the entire innovation chain of DigitalPET, with patent layouts distributed across eight countries and regions globally.

Zhang Bo believes that the current blossoming of digital PET clinical applications in multiple locations including Beijing, Anhui, Shandong, etc., fully demonstrates that as digital PET continues to break through in original innovation, its accumulated huge technological potential has transformed into extremely high industrial momentum, continuously injecting vitality into industry development.
