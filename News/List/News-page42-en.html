Domestic clinical all-digital PET/CT is allowed to enter the market

Wuhan, June 7 (Reporter Li Wei) The all-digital PET/CT invented by the team of Professor Xie Qingguo of Huazhong University of Science and Technology has recently passed the registration examination and approval of the State Drug Administration, and obtained market access and foreign sales qualifications...
2019-06-07
Xinhua News Agency

Wuhan, June 7 (Xinhua Li Wei) -- Professor Xie Qingguo's team from Huazhong University of Science and Technology has invented a fully digital PET/CT system. The device recently passed the registration approval by China's National Medical Products Administration, obtaining market access and sales qualifications. This signifies that domestically produced fully digital PET technology has broken through international technological monopolies, marking significant progress in the development of high-end medical equipment in China.

This was reported on June 7 by Huazhong University of Science and Technology. PET stands for Positron Emission Tomography (PET), a highly sensitive biochemical nuclear medicine molecular imaging technique. The PET/CT integrates CT with PET, providing anatomical location information from CT while offering functional and molecular information from PET, aiding in the early detection and precise diagnosis of malignant tumors and other lesions. Due to high technical barriers, its key technologies and equipment markets are mainly controlled by a few multinational corporations.

As the inventor of 'digital PET', Professor Xie Qingguo from Wuhan National Laboratory for Optoelectronics stated: “From scientific principle innovation to clinical whole machine equipment, and then obtaining market entry qualifications, it indicates that we have not only completed the transition from prototype to product (0 to 1), but also initiated the process of transforming products into commodities (1 to many).”

In January last year, the clinical fully digital PET/CT passed China's innovative medical device special approval, greatly accelerating its application in clinical settings. In July last year, the equipment completed clinical trial scans, validating its safety and efficacy for imaging diagnosis.

Professor Xiao Peng from Huazhong University of Science and Technology’s School of Life Sciences, a core member of the digital PET team, introduced that the concept of 'fully digital PET' was invented by their team in 2004. After more than ten years of development, they have established a complete technical system. Based on this foundation, the clinical fully digital PET/CT equipment uses fully independent intellectual property rights technology with the essential characteristics of 'full digitization' and 'precise sampling', fundamentally overturning traditional PET technological routes, capable of precisely detecting the smallest lesion sizes, significantly advancing cancer detection times.
