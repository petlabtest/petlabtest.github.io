"Key technology of small digital PET imaging" won the Hubei Technology Invention Award

News (Reporter Wu Hongyue) On February 25, the 2013 Hubei Provincial Science and Technology Award Ceremony was held...
2014-02-28
Science and Technology Daily

Science and Technology Daily reports (Reporter: Wu Hongyue) On February 25, the award ceremony for the Hubei Provincial Science and Technology Awards of 2013 was held. The research achievement 'Key Technologies for Small Digital PET Imaging' jointly submitted by Huazhong University of Science and Technology's PET Instrument Development and Multimodal Medical Imaging Laboratory and Suzhou Rayence Technology Co., Ltd. won the first prize in Hubei Province’s Technical Invention Award.
'Key Technologies for Small Digital PET Imaging', based on the MVT (Multi-Voltage Threshold) method independently invented by the PET laboratory, after years of research by dozens of researchers, successively completed technological innovations at three levels: electronics, detectors, and systems. They created new technologies with new knowledge, solving the world's challenging problem of digitalizing PET pulse signals for the first time from its root cause. Adhering to theoriginal aspirationof quickly converting new technology into products to benefit society, the laboratory did not stop at evaluating achievements based on patents and articles but faced actual and urgent social needs directly, making industrialization a higher-level goal. Suzhou Rayence Technology Co., Ltd., with its advanced product innovation concepts and solid social service spirit, became the greatest support for the laboratory in achieving this goal. The path from innovative methods to innovative technologies and then to innovative instruments for industrialization, guided by market demand and driven by enterprise standards, found the fastest direct channel. In 2010, the world's first fully digital PET prototype—Trans-PET—was unveiled through joint efforts of the laboratory and Rayence.
It is reported that 'Key Technologies for Digital PET Imaging' is the only research achievement in nuclear medicine imaging to receive this award among the Hubei Provincial Science and Technology Awards this year. Being able to stand out from numerous competitors in a province renowned for its education and science, winning such a high-value award, greatly encourages the laboratory and Rayence's practice of combining industry, academia, research, and application.
