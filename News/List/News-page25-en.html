Wuhan in Hubei Province Implements Comprehensive Digital Transformation Across All Industries

Boosting the Digital Economy to Drive Industrial Integration

By People's Daily reporters Tian Doudou and Wu Jun

People's Daily (April 13, 2021, Page 10)

Confirm patient positioning, press the start button, the examination table automatically enters the scanning position, and the detector begins to capture radiation data... At Ezhou Central Hospital in Hubei Province, the fully digital PET/CT (Positron Emission Tomography and X-ray Computed Tomography) equipment, known as the 'cancer early warning machine,' has been undergoing trial operation since last June. Through a series of precise parameters, it has detected cancer lesions for hundreds of patients in advance.

“The reputation of the fully digital PET/CT device is continuously rising; initially averaging one reception every four days, now there are an average of five people per day undergoing PET/CT examinations.” Professor Xie Qingguo, the inventor of the fully digital PET/CT and a professor at Huazhong University of Science and Technology, introduced that we have achieved leapfrog development from zero to one in innovative medical devices made in China. Next, we need to quickly enter the fast lane for development from one to many.

As one of the major achievements of Wuhan's smart healthcare initiative, the fully digital PET/CT is just one highlight among the city’s thriving digital economy. Currently, sectors such as intelligent transportation, smart logistics, online medical services, and online education are also rapidly developing. According to reports, in 2020, the added value of Wuhan's digital economy accounted for about 40% of the city's GDP, with a growth rate exceeding that of GDP by eight percentage points.

“To become one of China’s leading cities in the digital economy and an important node on the global digital industry chain, value chain, and innovation chain.” This is the goal set out in Wuhan's Implementation Plan for Breakthrough Development of Digital Economy.

Achieving this goal, Wuhan has confidence. In August 2019, the first batch of 5G base stations were put into use locally; currently, over 25,000 5G base stations have been built in Wuhan, achieving continuous high-quality coverage within the third ring road and key coverage in remote areas. The number of registrations for national industrial internet identification resolution top-level nodes is continuously growing, with space-based Internet of Things projects having started network formation, and a satellite tracking and control center officially established.

In addition to a strong foundation in digital economy, Wuhan also has clear plans: focusing on 'two transformations and three integrations,' that is, promoting the industrialization of digital technology and the digitization of industries, advancing the integration of manufacturing, services, and agriculture with digital technology.

“This is our lead product, Dongfeng Leading Navigation. Currently, over ten trial operation routes have been opened, with safe autonomous driving mileage exceeding 300,000 kilometers.” In the Dongfeng Motor Corporation Technical Center, the head of the unmanned vehicle project, Bian Ning, introduced to reporters the latest research results.

“During the '13th Five-Year Plan' period, Dongfeng Company has been dedicated to mastering autonomous key technologies for autonomous driving. Currently, our autonomous driving taxis are equipped with ‘5G+Beidou’ positioning, dynamic planning decision-making technology, and 'vehicle-road-network-cloud' collaborative technology, capable of achieving vehicle cloud monitoring, remote order dispatching, and human-vehicle interaction functions,” said Bian Ning.

Moreover, based on a robust digital economy infrastructure, Wuhan is also making continuous efforts in advantageous fields such as optoelectronics, chips, displays, terminals, and networks. A series of national-level innovation achievements are emerging in the field of digital economy: major breakthroughs have been made in key common technologies like 800G ultra-high-speed silicon photonics chips; the world's first 128-layer QLC flash memory chip has been launched, and China’s first flexible folding display production line has commenced operations.

Through informatization and digital construction, our central warehouse has adopted liti ku (stereoscopic warehouses) and automated sorting systems, establishing a rapid response logistics network. During peak periods, it can support daily order shipments of 150,000 to 200,000 units. Luo Yiqun, Deputy General Manager of the Information Technology Department at Liangpinpuzi Co., Ltd., said that the digital economy has now deeply 'embedded' into Wuhan residents’ lives. Industrial digitization is also rapidly advancing. Since last year, Wuhan has intensified efforts to implement intelligent transformation and upgrading for industrial enterprises, holding one promotional event per week. The 'Friday Appointment' for intelligent transformation has become a brand activity of Wuhan promoting new technological upgrades in enterprises.

Recently, the 2021 Spring Cherry Blossom Economic and Trade Negotiation Conference was held in Wuhan, with 112 projects signed at the conference, totaling an amount of 346.223 billion yuan. It is reported that most of these signed projects focus on Wuhan’s new generation information technology, high-end equipment manufacturing innovation, digital economy, and other fields, providing stronger momentum for the rapid development of Wuhan's digital economy.
