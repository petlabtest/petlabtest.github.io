# PETLab News Home Directions

Preview: `News/news_home-options.html`

## Source content structure

The design study uses the supplied `Home – Berkeley Lab News Center.webarchive` as a content-architecture reference. The archived home page identifies itself as a news-center landing page and contains a white main news area followed by a dedicated `featured-media-coverage` anchor and a themed media-coverage container. The study preserves that sequence in a PETLab context:

1. News masthead and topical navigation.
2. Primary featured story.
3. Latest-news listing.
4. In-the-media / featured coverage block.
5. Newsletter call to action.

All five directions use the same sample story hierarchy so comparison is about layout, not different content.

## Directions

### A. Editorial Briefing
White masthead, a large image/copy split, and a sober three-card feed. This borrows the readable split-panel and white-space rhythm used on PETLab research and about pages.

### B. Signal Grid
A deep-blue news masthead and photo-led feature panel with a floating copy block. This echoes PETLab's dark research bands and technology feature treatment while making the featured story the first visual signal.

### C. Journal
Large typography, a quiet featured editorial, and a compact ruled news list. This is closest to the publication and long-form research-page language, suitable when titles and recency should dominate over imagery.

### D. Mosaic
An image-first latest-news mosaic with blue overlays and an orange rule. It adapts the visual energy of PETLab project and application grids for a more magazine-like news destination.

### E. Impact Dispatch
High-contrast dark and orange channel blocks with deliberately compact headlines. It takes cues from PETLab impact/initiative bands and is best for a decisive, campaign-oriented newsroom presence.
