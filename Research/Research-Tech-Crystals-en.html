Scintillation Crystals

Programmable Crystals with Extreme Scintillation Performance

The scintillation crystal group focuses on two major systems: rare-earth scintillation crystals and perovskite scintillation crystals. It concentrates on cutting-edge issues such as the 4f-4f and 4f-5d energy level transition characteristics of rare-earth elements and the self-trapped emission mechanism of low-dimensional perovskite configurations. The group is dedicated to the design of programmable methods for novel scintillation luminescence and the development of scintillation crystal materials with ultimate performance. Oriented towards end-to-end innovation from high-performance scintillation crystal design to application, it provides an advanced R&D platform for outstanding students and top-tier talent, aiming to drive revolutionary changes in medical imaging fields like PET and CT from the core material level.

Featured Progress

Controlled Growth Technology for Scintillation Crystals

Centered on the core concepts of application orientation and AI empowerment, we are developing intelligent controlled growth technology for scintillation crystals to revolutionize the traditional R&D paradigm for crystal materials. We have developed multi-functional crystal growth furnaces and customized crystal growth software. By integrating density functional theory simulations and AI-assisted composition optimization, we achieve a closed technological loop of "theoretical design – growth control – performance feedback," supporting the customization from new crystal material design to application.

Programmable Scintillation Luminescence Methods

The rich energy levels of rare-earth elements harbor diverse luminescent information. Focusing on the 4f-4f and 4f-5d transition characteristics of rare-earth elements, we investigate the principles of energy level matching and crystal field modulation in rare-earth compounds to achieve programmable design of scintillation decay time and emission wavelength. Furthermore, we introduce the modulated scintillation luminescence information into front-end detection, aiming to break through the intrinsic performance limits of detectors.

Novel Configuration Perovskite Scintillation Crystals

Many scientific questions remain about the luminescence principles and crystallization mechanisms of perovskite scintillation crystals. Using the Atomistic Computing Platform, we elucidate the self-trapped emission mechanism in low-dimensional perovskite structures and investigate ultrafast scintillation mechanisms. Building on this work, we established a multifunctional melt-growth system and successfully grew centimeter-scale Cs(3)Cu(2)I(5) perovskite crystals, achieving a light output of 83,000 ph/MeV and an energy resolution of 4.8%. These results lay a foundation for stable growth and application of large perovskite single crystals.
