Advancing Scientific Breakthroughs and Industrial Translation Through Core Initiatives

Key Initiatives serve as the core driving force for the high-quality development of the laboratory. Centered on the national major strategic demands in fundamental sciences and medical imaging, these initiatives guide the laboratory to refine core research priorities, upgrade hardware infrastructure of scientific research platforms, and achieve breakthroughs in pivotal core technologies. Relying on such initiatives, the laboratory assembles high-caliber talents, deepens industry-university-research collaborative innovation, facilitates the generation and industrial translation of original research outcomes, consolidates disciplinary competitive advantages, and builds a distinctive academic brand. They deliver robust foundational support for the laboratory’s long-term advancement and high-standard construction.

Digital PET Imaging

All-Digital Silicon Photomultiplier with High Sensitivity and High Count Rate

High-sensitivity, high-throughput all-digital SiPM integrated circuits resolve overlapping scintillation signals under intense irradiation for high-rate PET and radiation detection environments.
