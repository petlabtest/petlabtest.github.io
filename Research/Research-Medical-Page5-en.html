Psychiatric Disorder Molecular Imaging

Psychiatric disorders including major depressive disorder, schizophrenia, bipolar disorder and substance use disorders impose enormous global disease burden, yet their diagnosis remains almost entirely reliant on subjective symptomatic assessment, with no validated objective biological markers. The underlying pathophysiology involves dysregulation of neurotransmitter systems, neuroinflammation, synaptic plasticity and functional neural circuits — changes that cannot be detected by structural brain imaging. PET molecular imaging enables direct in vivo measurement of neuroreceptor density, neurotransmitter release, neuroinflammatory status and synaptic function across brain regions, providing a window into the molecular mechanisms that drive psychiatric symptoms. This capability helps disentangle the biological heterogeneity within clinically defined diagnostic categories, identify objective biomarkers for early diagnosis and subtype stratification, and directly measure the central nervous system engagement of psychotropic medications. By grounding psychiatric diagnosis and treatment in measurable biological parameters, molecular psychiatry imaging moves the field toward biologically informed precision mental health care, reducing trial-and-error in treatment selection and accelerating the development of novel psychiatric therapeutics. Our group advances PET imaging paradigms for mental health disorders, deciphering the molecular basis of psychiatric conditions and supporting the development of targeted, mechanism-based treatments.

Digital PET Imaging

Digital PET imaging platforms deliver precise digitization for massive parallel channels and high-speed scintillation signal streams to achieve high-fidelity quantitative molecular measurement for nuclear medicine and particle physics experiments.
NSFC Original Exploration Program Project

All-Digital Silicon Photomultiplier with High Sensitivity and High Count Rate

High-sensitivity, high-throughput all-digital SiPM integrated circuits resolve overlapping scintillation signals under intense irradiation for high-rate PET and radiation detection environments.
NSFC National Major Scientific Research Instrument Development Project

Observation Device for Energy Transport of Proton Beams in Biological Tissues

Custom proton beam measurement instruments quantify beam energy deposition within biological tissues to enable real-time dosimetry monitoring and optimized proton therapy treatment planning.
NSFC Major International (Regional) Joint Research Project

Flat-panel PET Imaging

Flat-panel PET detection hardware expands molecular imaging workflows for intraoperative, preclinical small-animal and portable bedside scanning unavailable to conventional ring PET systems.
