Novel Instruments and Systems

Based on a fundamental technological revolution, the digital PET system achieves complete decoupling between detectors. Detection events are independently transmitted to the computing server, eliminating the traditional coincidence circuit and establishing a software-based coincidence architecture. Leveraging modular and open design, the PET system supports plug-and-play flexible expansion. Users can freely select and configure detector modules with different performance according to imaging needs, enabling the rapid assembly of various imaging systems such as proton therapy PET and helmet PET. Digital PET not only drives the evolution of PET imaging from general-purpose models towards application-specific use and precision diagnostics/therapy but also significantly expands its application boundaries in both clinical and research settings.
