Image Reconstruction

Harnessing the complete-sampling and comprehensive-data capabilities of All-Digital PET systems, the Image Reconstruction Research Group focuses on establishing the foundational reconstruction theory for these advanced detectors. The group develops intelligent reconstruction methods tailored for clinical PET, animal PET and specialized systems (including proton‐therapy PET and helmet PET). The research addresses both high-performance image reconstruction algorithms and full‐scope data‐correction strategies with the aim of driving PET performance metrics—time, energy, position and sensitivity—towards their physical limits. Simultaneously, the group explores frontier areas such as adaptive reconstruction, positronium imaging, prompt-gamma imaging and hybrid PET-SPECT reconstruction, opening up new directions in PET methodology. The Image Reconstruction Research Group continuously delivers algorithmic innovations to elevate the competitiveness of clinical and pre-clinical PET applications, persistently advancing accuracy and intelligence in PET reconstruction.

The Image Reconstruction team is currently seeking external funding, along with other institutional partners. Contact petlab@hust.edu.cn to inquire.
