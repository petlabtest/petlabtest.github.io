Chips for Ultra-Weak Light Detection

The Ultra-Weak Light Detection Chip Research Group specializes in the design and innovative application of next-generation silicon photomultiplier (SiPM) chips. It addresses the challenge of photon-signal digitization directly at the source, pushing weak-light detection devices toward their physical limits to enable the detection and characterization of single-photon-level event sequences.Recognizing the information-loss inherent in the traditional SiPM read-out chain — “Digital (pixel 0/1 output) → Analog (current-summation analog output) → Digital (ADC sampling)” — the group introduced the academic concept of Multi-Threshold Photon Detection, and developed a novel all-digital SiPM architecture based on a “Digital (pixel 0/1 output) → Digital (multi-threshold sampling)” paradigm. This shifts the sampling point from the aggregate SiPM output to the individual pixel level, and strives further to relocate it to the photon-generation site, thereby advancing toward time and spatial resolution approaching theoretical limits. The team places emphasis on developing demonstration applications in representative scenarios, continuously using these application demands to drive technological optimization and iterative enhancement — catalysing chain-reaction breakthroughs and innovation across related fields.

Featured Progress

  1. 2017

    First Standard CMOS SiPM

    The first SiPM product was developed and characterized using a 0.35 µm standard CMOS process.

  2. 2019

    43% Photon Detection Efficiency

    A photon detection efficiency of 43% was achieved using a 0.35 µm standard CMOS process.

  3. 2022

    Digital SiPM Prototype

    A digital SiPM prototype was developed, followed by testing and verification of its core technologies.

  4. 2024

    69% PDE with 0.11 µm CMOS

    In 2024, the PDE was further increased to 69% at 6 V overvoltage using a 0.11 μm standard CMOS process, achieving the best performance among current CMOS SiPMs. The single-photon time resolution of 50 ps and crosstalk probability below 5% surpass customized SiPM products and rank first worldwide.

  5. 2024

    World's First MT-SiPM Chip

    The team developed the first multi-threshold all-digital SiPM chip, introducing multi-threshold photon detection to reconstruct incident-photon time sequences at the microcell level.

Application implementation

Remote Sensing Satellites

The SiPM chips independently developed by the team served as core components onboard a satellite successfully launched into space. Operating under the harsh conditions of high cosmic ray radiation, they stably measured over 5 million valid data points in orbit.

Oil Exploration

The SiPM chips developed by the team serve as core components in petroleum logging tools, performing detection operations at depths of 2000 meters in oil wells.

Underwater Optical Communication

The SiPM chips independently developed by the team serve as core components for underwater optical communication systems, achieving weak optical signal reception amid complex turbidity and low-light marine conditions.

Application Exploration

SiPMs offer high performance, small size, and extreme insensitivity to magnetic fields, providing unique advantages over traditional photoelectric and imaging devices like Photomultiplier Tubes and CCDs. Based on our SiPM and MVT chips, we are conducting a series of novel optical imaging application explorations and demonstrations, such as spectrometers, low-light cameras, neutron imaging, and muon imaging, using application requirements to guide the direction of technological optimization.
