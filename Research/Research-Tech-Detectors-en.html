Modularized Particle Detectors

All-Counting, Fully Decoupled All-Digital Particle Detectors

The Particle Detector Research Group is committed to achieving breakthroughs in maximum-counting and complete-decoupling technical systems within the field of particle detection. Building on the original MVT method, it enables universal pulse acquisition and waveform digitisation while maintaining data-lightweighting, and it surpasses the accuracy limits of multi-parameter measurements—including time, energy and position—through high-precision physical-information reconstruction. By employing a modular, standardised design, the system is broken down into modules with standard interfaces and independent functionality, dramatically improving integration efficiency and maintainability, and facilitating automated calibration and flexible expansion. The team’s accomplishments have already been applied in domains such as medical imaging, security screening, petroleum exploration and high-energy physics, powerfully driving innovative development in radiation-detection methods and high-end instrumentation.

Featured Progress

Digital PET Detector

Traditional analog pulse processing relies on signal shaping and discrimination, which inherently suffer from temperature drift and non-linear distortion. In contrast, our research employs the Multi-Voltage Threshold (MVT) sampling method to digitize waveforms directly at the front end and then extracts event parameters via digital algorithms—eliminating distortions caused by analog circuitry. This fully digital strategy significantly improves the precision and stability of time, energy and position measurements while preserving a high counting rate, thereby offering a highly reliable and consistently performing solution for next-generation high-performance PET systems.


Prompt Gamma Detector

Conventional main control chips (e.g., ARM processors and FPGAs) struggle to maintain high-frequency operation under elevated temperatures, and high-speed ADCs also degrade in performance under such conditions. In neutron-logging operations, where tools must function at around 175 °C, cover an energy range of 0-14 MeV and handle prompt-gamma counting rates ≥ 1 Mcps, these limitations become critical. To address this, our research introduces the Multi-Voltage Threshold (MVT) method into the logging system. This approach requires neither a high-frequency clock nor pulse-stretching circuitry — the front-end uses just a small number of comparators and DACs, making it well suited for extreme temperature environments. The resulting MVT-based detector has achieved a single-channel count rate of ≥ 5 Mcps at 175 °C.


Radiation Detector

Historically, radiation-detector technology has been limited by a trade-off: extending the detection range via traditional ADC methods often reduces sensitivity. To address this limitation, the research integrates compact, high-gain silicon photomultipliers with scintillation crystals—yielding sensitivity improvements of one to two orders of magnitude compared with conventional Geiger counters and semiconductor detectors. This configuration results in a lower detection threshold, higher precision and a more compact design. On the signal-processing side, the proprietary Multi-Voltage Threshold (MVT) sampling method is employed to achieve high counting throughput while preserving high sensitivity—thereby extending the usable dose-rate range and overcoming the conventional compromise between sensitivity and range.

Resource Platform

MVT-Pi

MVT-Pi is a multi-channel, plug-and-play measurement platform with a compact design, high performance, and high reliability. Its core advantage lies in simultaneously achieving high-precision time measurement and amplitude measurement, enabling the precise capture and analysis of high-speed electrical signals. With its exceptional parallel processing capability, excellent signal integrity, and stable data transmission performance, MVT-Pi can provide an ideal hardware platform for critical areas such as scientific experiments, industrial inspection, and high-speed signal analysis.

Main Features

  • Product Dimensions: 110 mm × 140 mm × 50 mm
  • Time Measurement Accuracy: < 25 ps
  • Operating Power Consumption: < 10 W
  • Pulse Quantization Range: 0~4.1V
  • Threshold Step Accuracy: < 1 mV
  • Supports External Reset Control
  • Supported Channels: 6 (custom expansion supported)
  • Maximum Pulse Event Rate: 2 Mcps/ch
  • Supports External Clock Input
  • Configurable Number of Thresholds, supports up to 16 thresholds
  • Output Bandwidth: 1000 Mbps (custom expansion supported)
  • Supports Temperature Monitoring

MVT-Array

MVT-Array is a high-performance, modular multi-channel pulse digitization platform with exceptional reliability and signal processing capability. The system, based on a digital circuit architecture, achieves complete decoupling of signal conditioning and digitization circuits, allowing flexible configuration and supporting large-scale channel expansion. The MVT array acquisition system can efficiently acquire raw pulse information, providing a reliable hardware platform for research areas such as PET detector development, nuclear radiation research, X-ray industrial inspection, atmospheric transient luminous events, and cosmic ray observation.

Main Features

  • Product Size: 273 mm × 135 mm × 107 mm
  • Threshold Step Accuracy: < 1 mV
  • Supports External Reset Control
  • Pulse Quantization Range: 0~1.8V
  • Maximum Pulse Event Rate: 2 Mcps/ch
  • Supports External Clock Input
  • Number of Supported Channels: 576
  • Output Bandwidth: 1000 Mbps (expandable to 10 Gbps)
  • Supports Temperature Monitoring
  • Configurable Number of Thresholds, up to 8 thresholds
  • Operating Power Consumption: < 120 W
  • Supports Overcurrent and Overvoltage Protection
  • Time Measurement Accuracy: < 25 ps
  • Supports Input Pulse Baseline Offset Adjustment
  • Supports Unique ID Setting
