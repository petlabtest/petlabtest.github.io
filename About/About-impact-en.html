World Intellectual Property Organization (WIPO) Global Award - Inaugural Edition

One of only five projects globally to receive this award

The WIPO Global Award is a global recognition scheme launched by the World Intellectual Property Organization (WIPO). It is established to honor enterprises and individuals worldwide that have delivered positive contributions to economic, social and cultural advancement both domestically and internationally through the application of intellectual property.

The Multi-Voltage Threshold (MVT) sampling technology, independently developed by the research team, has addressed the long-standing global challenge of precise sampling of ultra-high-speed positron emission tomography (PET) pulses. Building on this foundational breakthrough, the team pioneered all-digital PET imaging and constructed a complete innovation chain spanning from basic theories and core technologies to key components and full system integration — a feat that constitutes a veritable "reinvention" of PET technology. The team has subsequently developed and launched a series of landmark world-first innovations, including the world’s first all-digital radiation detection system, the world’s first all-digital preclinical animal PET scanner, and the world’s first all-digital clinical PET system.

China National Invention Patent Gold Award

The Only One in the PET Field

The China Patent Award is the only governmental award in China specifically established to recognize patented inventions and creations. As the highest distinction in China’s patent sector, it is co-presented by the China National Intellectual Property Administration (CNIPA) and the World Intellectual Property Organization (WIPO).

The award-winning patent has broken through critical technological barriers and inaugurated the era of all-digital PET. By addressing the long-standing global challenge of precise sampling of ultra-high-speed scintillation pulse signals, this technology has effectively propelled PET from the "film camera era" into the "digital camera era", exerting industry-leading influence in the domains of PET imaging and radiation detection.

Specifically, digitalization has freed PET systems from closed system architectures, enabling the development of dedicated regional PET devices, including dedicated brain PET scanners, proton therapy PET systems, and endoscopic PET systems. This portfolio of dedicated PET instruments will serve as powerful enablers for clinical research, advancing cutting-edge basic investigations into the physiology, pathology, diagnosis, and clinical management of major diseases such as brain disorders and malignancies.

Furthermore, the patented technology demonstrates strong technological versatility, with applications extending beyond the medical field to a wide range of sectors including nuclear radiation detection, petroleum exploration, and security inspection CT systems.
