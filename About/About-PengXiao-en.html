Focuses on imaging methodology research and instrument development.

The work revolves around two key aspects: "signal processing and image reconstruction for PET (Positron Emission Tomography) data streams" and "intelligent, application-adaptive PET system design." This has led to the realization of a series of innovative systems and their novel applications—such as "dual-dynamic imaging PET" and "radiotherapy navigation PET"—characterized by their uniqueness ("what others do not have") and superior performance ("excelling where others exist"). The affiliated research team, centering on intelligent detection and imaging, conducts research spanning the entire intelligent innovation chain: from key scintillator materials and core photoelectric devices to intelligent imaging systems and application studies; and from high-energy particle signal detection and signal processing to image reconstruction and image processing. This has established a globally top-tier industry-academia-research-application platform integrating talent cultivation, technological innovation, and industrial support.

Has successively presided over one project each for the National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC) Young Scientists Fund, Emergency Management Project, General Program, and National Major Scientific Research Instrument and Equipment Development Project; as well as one project and one sub-project for the National Key Research and Development Program of China. Has published over 70 papers in relevant journals and conferences such as IEEE Transactions on Radiation and Plasma Medical Sciences, Physics in Medicine and Biology, and the IEEE Nuclear Science Symposium and Medical Imaging Conference (NSS/MIC). Has filed a total of 85 domestic and international patent applications, with 34 already granted. Related work and achievements have received two Hubei Province Technology Invention First Prizes (2013, 2024, as the second contributor) and one Huang Jiasi Biomedical Engineering Award First Prize (2019, as the second contributor). The team's related achievements were selected for "China's Top 10 Scientific and Technological Advances News of 2019" and "China's Major Medical Advances of 2019."

Recruitment Information

Based on the aforementioned research directions, We recruits 1 doctoral student, 2 master's students, and 3 undergraduate students annually. Prospective students are expected to possess solid foundational knowledge in mathematics and physics, along with strong programming skills. Prior experience in mathematical modeling, software-hardware co-development, or medical image processing will be advantageous. We provide students with a first-class research environment and platform resources.
