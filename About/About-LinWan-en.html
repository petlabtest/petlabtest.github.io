Focuses on building the Plug-n-Image technology system to support the rapid construction and innovative applications of detection and imaging systems.

Focusing on the construction of the Plug-n-Image technical system, this study explores the standardized and modular core operators and access mechanisms for the full-process computing from detection to imaging, as well as the AI-assisted methods for medical image feature extraction and clinical cognition acquisition. The Plug-n-Image computing architecture has been successfully applied to the rapid construction of scientific research instruments such as proton knife PET and dual dynamic imaging PET. We have developed the Rayplus intelligent medical image analysis and processing platform and the real-world evidence clinical research platform, supporting multiple hospitals in carrying out multi-center clinical trials. With years of teaching experience in courses such as programming and software engineering and project practice experience, I have been continuously researching programming methods and software development paradigms, and published the translated work "The Art of Functional Programming".

Presided over 1 key R&D program project of the Ministry of Science and Technology, 1 international (regional) cooperation and exchange project of the National Natural Science Foundation of China, and more than 10 military-civilian horizontal projects. Applied for a total of 12 domestic and foreign patents, with 7 authorized, and published more than 30 papers. The relevant work has won the First Prize of the New Engineering Major Senior Title Group in the 4th National University Teachers' Teaching Innovation Competition (2024, the 1st completer), the National First-Class Undergraduate Course (2023, *Computer Graphics*, the 1st completer), etc., and was selected into the "2021 Excellent Teacher Reward Program for Computer Majors in National Universities".
