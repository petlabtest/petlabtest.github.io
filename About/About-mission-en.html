Our mission

Led by Professor Qingguo Xie, the Intelligent Detection and Imaging Laboratory brings together scientists from China, Italy, Russia, Finland, and other countries, spanning more than ten disciplines including biomedical engineering, telecommunications, electronics, control systems, computer science, mathematics, mechanical engineering, nuclear medicine, radiology, and clinical medicine. In 2001, the team pioneered the new research direction of digital PET. After two decades of development, and supported by a robust scientific research platform, it has gradually evolved into a top global platform integrating talent cultivation, technological innovation, and industrial application.

Through the invention of the Multi-Voltage Threshold (MVT) sampling method, which solved the challenge of precise digitization of high-speed pulses, the team has systematically advanced all-digital, modular PET detector technology and a series of intelligent imaging methods. It has conducted in-depth research into the scientific principles and technological innovations underlying the five mathematical-physical processes in PET (from matter to energy, radiation to light, light to electricity, analog to data, and data to image).

The world's first MVT sampling method has achieved versatility across multiple fields. In medical imaging, it has successively led to the completion of: The world's first all-digital PET scientific instrument for animals (Trans-PET BioCaliburn), The world's first clinical all-digital PET medical device (D-PET100), The world's first standard CMOS silicon photomultiplier (TP3050), The world's first brain-dedicated all-digital PET system (DigitMI i30), The world's first proton beam PET system. All these achievements are supported by complete independent intellectual property rights. This work has changed the evaluation standards within the PET industry and ushered in a new era of all-digital PET. In the field of high-energy radiation detection, including petroleum exploration, radiation detection, security inspection equipment, and nuclear fusion neutron detectors, technological breakthroughs and successful application of results have also been realized.

The team has achieved a complete innovation cycle, from scientific discovery and technological invention to application development. Results have been translated across the entire industrial chain, including: key materials (high-performance scintillation crystals), key components (ultra-weak light detection chips), core modules (modular particle detectors), core software (end-to-end software platforms), and complete systems (all-digital PET scientific instruments for animals and all-digital PET medical devices for clinical use). Cumulatively, the team has applied for over 600 patents, with more than 240 granted, covering the entire technology chain from electronics, detectors, and system integration to software algorithms and products – ranking first globally in quantity within the field.

Relying on major scientific installations such as the Digital PET Brain Imaging Platform and the Super Digital PET Imaging Platform, the team actively expands new applications, discovers new principles, and accumulates new data. With a mission to explore the origins of life, consciousness, and the cosmos, it contributes intellectual power to benefit human health and advance scientific development.

National Digital PET R&D Center

We are defined by our role as the nation’s core research and development center dedicated to digital PET innovation, uniquely combining state-of-the-art imaging R&D with rapid hardware prototyping and system integration capabilities. We focus on the end-to-end development of transformative imaging capabilities that cannot be delivered as effectively by standalone clinical institutions or commercial industry partners alone. Once technologies are matured, we work in close coordination with national health authorities and research governing bodies to transfer them directly to frontline clinical institutions and industry partners for clinical deployment and scaled production. 

This end-to-end innovation pathway is central to our public-oriented research mission, which delivers distinct advantages to national healthcare advancement and the broader medical ecosystem. 

  • Enduring strategic partnership: We operate as a long-term strategic partner to national health authorities and clinical networks, sustaining continuity of deep expertise in critical digital PET domains, responding nimbly to urgent clinical and public health needs, and sharing our technical knowledge with the wider medical research community.
  • Innovation as Our Core Culture: We believe our pioneering advances in digital PET technology are rooted in the free exchange of ideas, the willingness to challenge conventions, the freedom to take exploratory risks, the agility to respond to evolving clinical needs, and the ambition to deliver meaningful impact. We foster and protect this innovative culture across our laboratory, empowering our team to pursue transformative scientific breakthroughs.
  • Objective, trusted expertise: We serve solely in the public interest and national healthcare strategy as a trusted, unbiased source of technological capabilities, accelerating the pace of innovation required to address unmet clinical needs and advance precision medicine at scale.
