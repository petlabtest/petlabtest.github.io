Qingguo Xie

Professor and Researcher at the Wuhan National Laboratory for Optoelectronics, Department of Biomedical Engineering, School of Life Science and Technology, Huazhong University of Science and Technology. Inventor of digital PET, recipient of the National Science Fund for Distinguished Young Scholars.

  • Department: Department of Biomedical Engineering, School of Life Science and Technology, Huazhong University of Science and Technology
  • Address: Room 410, Enming Building, Huazhong University of Science and Technology
  • Email: qgxie@hust.edu.cn

Focuses on research of detection and imaging methodologies and instrument development.

The Digital PET Laboratory was founded at Huazhong University of Science and Technology in 2001, where the Multi-Voltage Threshold sampling method was invented. This method has been applied in fields such as medical devices, scientific instruments, radiation detection, and petroleum exploration logging. In the fields of medical devices and scientific instruments, it has solved the problem of precise digitization of high-speed pulses, systematically developed fully digital and modular PET detector technologies as well as a series of intelligent imaging methods, and established a systematic and complete fully digital PET technical system. Leading the team, the laboratory has achieved four world-first innovations, including the fully digital PET scientific instrument (2010), fully digital PET medical device (2015), standard CMOS silicon photomultiplier (2017), and brain-specific fully digital PET (2019). It is also carrying out cutting-edge application exploration and demonstration.

Founded and led an international scientific research team to conduct over 20 years of systematic research, covering the complete innovation chain from key materials, core devices, and imaging methods to scientific instruments, medical equipment, and cutting-edge applications. Pioneered a new direction in digital PET imaging and built a world-top-tier global产学研 integration platform integrating talent cultivation, scientific discovery, technological innovation, and industrial development.

He successively presided over one project each of the National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC) including the Youth Science Fund Project, General Program, Special Fund for Basic Scientific Research of Scientific Instruments, Major International (Regional) Joint Research Project, Distinguished Young Scholars Fund Project, and National Major Scientific Instrument Development Project; one project each of the National High-Tech R&D Program (863 Program) and the National Major Scientific Instrument and Equipment Development Special Project; and two projects of the National Special Program for International Scientific and Technological Cooperation. He has published more than 140 papers in journals such as *IEEE Transactions on Nuclear Science*, *Physics in Medicine and Biology*, *IEEE Journal of the Electron Devices Society*, *Nature Medicine*, *Nature Photonics*, and *Nature Communications*. As an inventor, he has applied for over 300 invention patents and obtained more than 100 authorized patents covering regions including China, the United States, Germany, and Japan.

Related work has been selected for "China's Top 10 Scientific and Technological Advances News of 2019" and "China's Major Medical Advances of 2019." Was awarded the title of "Science and Technology Gala—CCTV 2019 Annual Science and Technology Innovation Figure." Has successively received the Hubei Province Technology Invention First Prize (2013, 2024), three Geneva International Inventions Exhibition Gold Medals (2013, 2017), the Huang Jiasi Biomedical Engineering Award Technology Invention First Prize (2019), the China Patent Gold Award (2021), and the WIPO Global Award (2022).
