About Us

Origin of All-Digital PET

The Digital PET Laboratory is committed to cultivating global leaders in frontier research on digital medical imaging. Its vision is to achieve transformative applications of digital PET in early diagnosis, precision treatment, and drug development. By addressing needs in the life sciences and medicine, the Laboratory seeks to visualize the previously unseen, shape the future, and accelerate progress against major diseases such as cancer and neurological disorders.

Mission and Vision

Provide digital solutions for molecular imaging

Centered on original all-digital PET technologies, the laboratory devotes itself to full-chain digital innovation in molecular imaging. It has established a complete technical system covering core components, complete systems, data algorithms, clinical applications and research services, providing end-to-end, full-process and high-performance all-digital solutions for precise diagnosis of major diseases, new drug development and life science research worldwide.

Adhering to original innovation, independent controllability, full-industry-chain integration and value-driven development, we break through the bottlenecks of conventional analog technologies. With core competencies in digital photon detection, high-speed pulse sampling, intelligent image reconstruction and quantitative precise analysis, we realize full-link digitalization of molecular-level signals ranging from detection, acquisition, transmission and processing to visualization.Targeting major diseases including oncology, neurological disorders and cardiovascular diseases, we deliver digital molecular imaging equipment and technical services featuring ultra-high definition imaging, ultra-high sensitivity, ultra-fast scanning, ultra-low radiation dose and precise quantification. Our achievements facilitate early diagnosis and early treatment in clinical practice, drive breakthrough innovations in scientific research, and boost independent upgrading of relevant industries.

Our Impact

An innovation journey over two decades

Since pioneering the new research direction of digital PET in 2001, the Digital PET Laboratory has consistently expanded research frontiers through technological breakthroughs. For more than two decades, we have upheld the mission of exploring the unknown, breaking technical bottlenecks and empowering industrial development. We have progressed from fundamental research accumulation to iterative breakthroughs in core technologies, and from in-depth exploration in single fields to coordinated full-chain innovation. Every research effort is translated into tangible strength to advance disciplinary development and serve national strategic needs. We consolidate core strength over years and fulfill our mission through persistent innovation.
