About Us

Where the Laboratory Began

Led by Professor Qingguo Xie, the Digital PET Laboratory brings together scientists from China, Italy, Russia, Finland, and other countries. Its work spans more than ten disciplines, including biomedical engineering, telecommunications, electronics, control, computer science, mathematics, mechanical engineering, nuclear medicine, radiology, and clinical medicine. The team opened a new direction in digital PET research in 2001. Over more than two decades, supported by strong research platforms, it has developed into a globally leading ecosystem integrating talent development, technological innovation, and industrial application.

Digitizing Electrical Signals at the Source

By inventing the multi-voltage threshold sampling method, or MVT, the team solved the challenge of precisely digitizing high-speed pulses. It systematically developed all-digital, modular PET detector technologies and a series of intelligent imaging methods, while advancing the scientific principles and technical innovation behind five mathematical and physical processes in PET: matter to energy, radiation to light, light to electricity, analog signals to data, and data to images.

Original Technologies and Representative Systems

The world's first MVT sampling method has been applied across multiple fields. In medical imaging, the team developed the world's first all-digital PET instrument for animal research, Trans-PET BioCaliburn; the first clinical all-digital PET system, D-PET100; the first standard CMOS silicon photomultiplier, TP3050; the first dedicated all-digital brain PET system, DigitMI i30; and the first proton-therapy PET system. The team owns the complete intellectual property portfolio for these technologies. This work changed how PET performance is evaluated and opened the era of all-digital PET. The Laboratory has also achieved technical breakthroughs and applications in high-energy radiation detection, including petroleum exploration, radiation monitoring, security screening, and fusion-neutron detection.

A Complete Innovation System

The team has established a complete innovation cycle from scientific discovery and technological invention to applied development. Results have been translated across the entire value chain: key materials such as high-performance scintillation crystals, key devices such as ultra-low-light detector chips, core components such as intelligent modular particle detectors, core software through an end-to-end software platform, and complete systems for both animal research and clinical use. More than 600 patent applications and over 240 granted patents cover electronics, detectors, system integration, software algorithms, and products, ranking first worldwide in the field by volume.

Our Long-Term Mission

Building on major research infrastructure such as digital PET brain-imaging platforms and super digital PET imaging platforms, the team continues to explore new applications, discover new patterns, and accumulate new data. Its mission is to investigate the origins of the universe, life, and consciousness while contributing to human health and scientific progress.
