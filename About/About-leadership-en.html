Our Leadership

Qingguo Xie

Chinese national, Ph.D. Professor and doctoral supervisor of Wuhan National Laboratory for Optoelectronics and Huazhong University of Science and Technology, as well as the inventor of all-digital PET technology. His research focuses on methodological exploration and instrument development for Positron Emission Tomography (PET). In 2001, he established the Digital PET Laboratory at Huazhong University of Science and Technology. He has achieved systematic innovations in pulse digitization methodologies, detector technologies, and imaging system architectures, accomplishing a comprehensive technological leap from conceptual proposition and fundamental principles to mature system implementation and practical application.

Peng Xiao

Chinese national, Ph.D. Professor and doctoral supervisor of Huazhong University of Science and Technology. Focuses on signal processing and image reconstruction oriented to PET data flow, as well as the design of intelligently adaptive PET systems. A list-mode-data-based regularized relaxed ordered subsets (LMROS) algorithm is proposed to address the challenge of sparse-data reconstruction, which enables high-quality image reconstruction under low-count and truncated-data conditions. Targeted at emerging practical applications, he has developed a series of flexible and open all digital PET systems, achieving high-performance imaging customized and adaptable for diverse application scenarios.

Nicola D' Ascenzo

Italian national, Ph.D. Professor and doctoral supervisor of Huazhong University of Science and Technology. Focuses on photon response mechanisms and signal processing methods in single-photon detection chips, as well as novel PET system design and imaging methodologies (including helmet PET and Agri-PET). Dedicated to the development of all-digital silicon photomultipliers (SiPM) and portable PET imaging equipment.

Lin Wan

Chinese national, Ph.D. Professor and doctoral supervisor of Huazhong University of Science and Technology. Focuses on establishing the Plug-n-Image technical system to facilitate the rapid development and innovative application of detection and imaging systems. Centering on the construction of this system, exploring standardized and modular core operators as well as access mechanisms for full-process computation ranging from detection to imaging, alongside AI-enabled methods for medical imaging feature extraction and clinical cognition acquisition.

Rui Zheng

Chinese national, Ph.D.

Associate Researcher, School of Information Science and Technology, University of Science and Technology of China.

Focuses on novel materials and methods for single-photon radiation detection. Concentrating on the development of new scintillation luminescence mechanisms and scintillation crystal materials with ultimate performance.

Bingxuan Li

Chinese national, Ph.D.

Associate Researcher, Artificial Intelligence Research Institute, Hefei Comprehensive National Science Center.

Chief Scientist of National Key R&D Program of China; One of the main drafters of China's digital PET industry standards. Focuses on novel PET imaging system design and medical image reconstruction.

Daoming Xi

Chinese national, Ph.D.

Associate Researcher, Artificial Intelligence Research Institute, Hefei Comprehensive National Science Center.

Focuses on the design of photon-counting X-ray detectors and the development of imaging systems. Proposed the FPGA-only MVT digitizer technical solution and designed the corresponding circuit system.
