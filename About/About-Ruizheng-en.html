Rui Zheng

Associate Researcher, Department of Electronic Engineering and Information Science (Department 6), School of Information Science and Technology, University of Science and Technology of China.

  • Affiliation: Department of Electronic Engineering and Information Science, School of Information Science and Technology, University of Science and Technology of China

  • Email: zhengrui@ustc.edu.cn

Focuses on novel materials and methods for single-photon radiation detection, conducting research on new scintillator materials, single-photon radiation detection, and imaging.

Has established an end-to-end technology platform from the design to application of high-performance scintillator crystals, providing core material support for radiation detection fields including all-digital PET and photon-counting CT. Concentrates on developing novel scintillation mechanisms and scintillator materials with ultimate performance, driving transformative advancements in imaging fields such as PET and CT through fundamental material innovation.

Has published over 10 high-quality international papers in related journals and conferences including CrystEngComm, Journal of Crystal Growth, and IEEE Transactions on Radiation and Plasma Medical Sciences, and has filed more than 10 domestic and international patent applications. Awarded the Hubei Province Technology Invention First Prize for "Key Technologies of All-Digital PET" (ranked 5th). Has led one project under the National Key R&D Program, served as task lead for a collaborating institution in one National Major Scientific Research Instrument Development Project, and co-led one 2022 Anhui Higher Education Institutions Collaborative Innovation Project.
