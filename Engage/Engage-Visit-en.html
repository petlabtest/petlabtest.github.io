Visitor Requirements

All visits must be pre-coordinated with an appropriate Digital PET Laboratory host prior to arrival. Please work with your respective host on any program-specific prerequisites, outside of the classified visit certification process outlined below, that may be needed prior to your arrival.

Visiting the Laboratory for a Classified Meeting

Prior to arrival for any classified visits, a classified visit certification from your host organization must be on file with the Laboratory’s Visitor Services Center.

Please allow 48 hours for all visit requests from the host organization to be processed by Digital PET Laboratory.

Standard Visit Submission Format

  • Visit Name: [Last Name of Visitor(s)]
  • Visit Reason: Meeting
  • Access Level: [Select Level of Meeting]
  • POC Name/Phone Number: [Visit Host POC for Meeting]
  • Start Date: [Select Date of Meeting]
  • End Date: [No Later Than a Year From the Start Date]

NOTE: It is important the individual submitting the certification list the Visit Name in the format specified above.

Check In & ID Requirements

When you check in with the Visitor Services Center, you will be asked a series of questions related to your visit in your possession. You will be required to show government-issued photo identification. For national citizens, this identification will be an ID card, or Passport. For Foreign Nationals, this identification will be a Passport from country of origin.
