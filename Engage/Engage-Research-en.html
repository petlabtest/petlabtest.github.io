Research Collaboration

Digital PET Laboratory's status as a large interdisciplinary research and development center makes it a strong resource for collaborative research initiatives.

We deliver extensive technical expertise to academic researchers from both internal and external institutions, covering faculty members and student researchers alike. Our ability to architect and build detectors that enable significant science research is well established. The synergy between institutional academic research strengths and the Laboratory’s experience in prototype development has driven innovative discoveries in frontier science.


Collaborations with PETLab

Digital PET Laboratory maintains long-term research and development partnerships with numerous domestic and international universities across a broad spectrum of interdisciplinary research fields. Our collaborative scope covers core disciplines including Physical Science, Life Science, Nuclear Science and Pharmaceutical Science. Joint research activities span fundamental theoretical exploration, equipment prototype iteration, clinical translational verification and innovative technology transformation, integrating the laboratory’s advantages in full-digital PET detector design with the academic strengths of partner universities. As a cross-disciplinary research platform, we continuously launch joint research projects, co-supervise postgraduate students and exchange technical personnel with university teams to jointly advance breakthroughs in cutting-edge biomedical and nuclear detection technologies.


COLLABORATION AGREEMENTS

The Collaboration Agreement (CA) serves as the formal framework through which our nationally funded research institute builds long-term cooperative ties with non-profit organizations, universities and governmental departments. All partnerships established under this framework center on the core mission of boosting interdisciplinary scientific research and innovative detection technology development.

A collaboration agreement outlines the purpose and objectives of the project and establishes the roles and responsibilities of the parties. Digital PET Laboratory enters into CAs when the collaborative research supports its mission and program objectives.

Partnering with our laboratory via a Collaboration Agreement enables your institution to advance its research agenda and deliver impactful scientific outcomes through access to our proprietary technologies, specialized technical know-how, and state-of-the-art research facilities. These core resources encompass the Advanced Biomedical Imaging Facility (ABIF), universal research platforms, and a comprehensive array of scientific instrumentation.

Among the research areas we are interested in are physical science, life science, nuclear science and pharmaceutical science. If you believe a collaborative R&D project will benefit your institution and our R&D efforts, start by contacting us at PETLAB@hust.edu.cn.

CONSIDERATIONS WHEN CONTEMPLATING A COLLABORATION AGREEMENT

  • Intellectual property (IP)

    PETLab provides a license that allows access to and use of any prior IP necessary for the partner to perform the R&D outlined in the CA. IP created by either partner during the CA is owned solely by that partner; IP created jointly is owned jointly. In most cases, the CA-generated IP is subject to a Government Use License.

  • Funding

    CAs are typically funded by the partner. Work on the project cannot begin until a minimum of 60-day advance funding is received from the partner.
