Research Collaboration

Explore with Digital PET Laboratory

We welcome universities, research institutes, medical organizations, and industry partners seeking long-term, open, and responsible collaboration around shared scientific questions. Each collaboration begins with a clear research objective and advances through joint project definition, appropriate access to research resources, and coordinated execution toward verifiable scientific discovery and clinical translation.

Collaboration from platform to prototype

Our research platforms, digital detection and imaging technologies, prototype systems, experimental facilities, and interdisciplinary team can provide technical and personnel support suited to the goals of each project.

Collaboration process

Turn a shared question into a joint project

  1. Submit a collaboration need
  2. Hold an initial discussion
  3. Define the project together
  4. Confirm agreements and boundaries
  5. Conduct the research and manage outcomes

Specific agreements, intellectual-property terms, and research-output arrangements are governed by formally authorized university and laboratory documents.
