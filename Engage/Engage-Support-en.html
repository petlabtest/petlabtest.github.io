ABOUT TECHNICAL SUPPORT

Digital PET Laboratory draws on its work in all-digital PET, low-light detection, fast-pulse digitization, and medical image computing to support universities, hospitals, and industry partners facing practical technical problems. Support begins with requirements analysis and may extend to detector and electronics design, imaging-software configuration, experimental testing, data analysis, and training. Our technical support follows three principles:

  • Define the research, preclinical, or engineering question together with measurable criteria
  • Combine the Laboratory’s software, detectors, readout electronics, and imaging platforms for validation
  • Provide traceable data, technical documentation, and training so results can be understood and reused

Support Areas

Software and Data

Support for imaging-protocol configuration, pulse processing, correction, and reconstruction, including project-specific data processing and algorithm evaluation.

Detectors and Electronics

Component selection, module design, and performance tuning for scintillation crystals, silicon photomultipliers, front-end readout, and multi-voltage-threshold digitization.
Imaging Systems and Testing

Measurement of timing, energy, and spatial performance using Laboratory research platforms, with support for dedicated PET prototypes, protocols, and application validation.

Training and Consulting

Technical-route assessment, experimental-design review, software guidance, and focused training to help teams establish reproducible research and testing workflows.


Technical Support Process

  1. Define the Problem

    Describe the application, technical challenge, and expected result

    Summarize available equipment, data, and schedule constraints

    Identify whether consulting, development, testing, or training is needed

  2. Contact Us

    Submit a requirements summary and necessary non-sensitive technical material

    The Laboratory assesses feasibility, interfaces, and required resources

  3. Plan the Work

    Agree on scope, deliverables, evaluation criteria, and milestones

    Schedule software configuration, module development, or experimental testing

    Confirm conditions for data, samples, and specialized facilities

    Establish technical contacts and review points

  4. Deliver and Follow Up

    Complete the agreed analysis, development, testing, or training

    Provide results and supporting documentation, then iterate from feedback
