Research for Core National Strategic Demands

Since our founding in 2004, the Digital PET Laboratory has centered its research on national strategic priorities, key industrial bottlenecks, and major public health challenges. By building a full-chain innovation system covering basic research, technological innovation, clinical translation and industrial application, the Laboratory advances high-level sci-tech self-reliance and serves national strategic development.

Our impact

From photons to a clearer picture of life

The Digital PET Laboratory develops foundational technologies and advances all-digital PET innovation, evolving general-purpose imaging architectures into specialized systems and precision diagnosis and treatment solutions, while continually expanding the applications of PET in clinical care and frontier research.
