Research Assistant Development Framework

Purpose of the framework

The laboratory is developing a Research Assistant (RA) framework to identify and support students who demonstrate strong academic curiosity, sound research and practical ability, responsibility, initiative, alignment with the laboratory's vision, and a genuine willingness to contribute. Its goal is to create shared value through both the laboratory's sustained development and each student's personal growth.

RA levels will serve as a core mechanism for talent development. They will reflect a student's combined knowledge, skills, research conduct, and values, and will provide the basis for access to laboratory resources.
