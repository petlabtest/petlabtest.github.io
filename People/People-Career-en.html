Career Opportunities

PETLab is now recruiting postdoctoral researchers globally. Outstanding young talents aspiring to dedicate themselves to scientific research and become leaders in fields such as digital PET research, intelligent detection, and software algorithms are welcome to join!

Application Requirements:

  • A background in materials, chemistry, optoelectronic engineering, physics, electronics, automation, computer science, telecommunications, biomedical engineering, biology, pharmacy, clinical medicine, radiology, nuclear medicine, or related fields.
  • A Ph.D. obtained within the last 3 years.
  • Age under 35.
  • Experience independently leading scientific research or serving as a key researcher in relevant professional projects.
  • Teamwork spirit and capabilities, strong communication skills, and a commitment to quality and results.
  • Professional English listening, speaking, reading, and writing skills, with fluent oral expression.
  • A strong interest in scientific research.

 

Work Locations and Benefits:

  • Hefei, Wuhan, Shenzhen (China); Chicago (USA); Pozzuoli/Caserta (Italy); Magdeburg (Germany).
  • Appointment period of 2-6 years, with phased overseas training (USA, Finland, Italy, Germany). Outstanding candidates may secure formal positions in research or industry.
  • Salary: Negotiable.
