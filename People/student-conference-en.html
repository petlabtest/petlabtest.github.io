2024.11.18

Attended the 6th International Workshop on New Photon-Detectors (PD24) in Canada

Shared the latest progress in CMOS SiPM chip development and applications.

To promote application demonstrations and explore new applications, Digital PETer and doctoral student Sheng Laohui attended the meeting in Canada. They exchanged views with experts from leading international teams, including CERN, Fermilab, and the Institute of High Energy Physics of the Chinese Academy of Sciences, and received valuable feedback on the team’s technical roadmap and potential collaboration. They also learned about the latest developments in particle detectors, digital SiPMs, and other frontier areas, providing useful input for the team’s future technical iterations.

2024.11.17

Advanced the animal all-digital PET/CT collaboration at Turku PET Centre in Finland

Deployed the new PnI software architecture on the E180 animal all-digital PET/CT system.

Digital PETer, doctoral students Ling Yiqing, Li Ang, and Li Ximeng, and master’s students Liu Zixiao and Chen Mengxin visited Finland. They discussed digital PET technology with their Finnish collaborators and developed quality-control and calibration procedures for the system. Phantom and animal comparison experiments verified the stability of the PnI software and the accuracy of the core PET/CT algorithms. Building on these results, the teams discussed application studies and potential joint projects, and planned to apply for research funding together, laying a foundation for long-term collaboration and technology transfer.

2024.10.19

Attended the 37th Congress of the European Association of Nuclear Medicine (EANM) in Germany

Discussed the potential and future directions of brain all-digital PET.

Digital PETer and doctoral student Zhang Weirui attended the meeting in Germany, presenting the significant resolution and sensitivity advantages of brain all-digital PET and its clinical applications in cerebral blood-flow imaging. They reached preliminary cross-disciplinary collaboration intentions with experts. The opening ceremony and keynote sessions also discussed integrating nuclear medicine with precision medicine to provide more personalized diagnosis and treatment, reflecting current challenges and topics in nuclear medicine.

2023.10.16

Hosted the China-Germany Workshop on Digital PET Technology and Molecular Nuclear Medicine Imaging at HUST

Explored the potential of all-digital PET with multimodal CT and MRI methods.

Supported by the China-Germany cooperation and exchange project of the NSFC Research Exchange Center, the workshop was hosted by Huazhong University of Science and Technology and the University of Science and Technology of China, and organized by Raycan Medical. Academic experts in PET systems and applications from China and Germany explored the potential of all-digital PET with multimodal CT and MRI methods, aiming to advance interventional technology. Raycan Medical and Raycan Technology presented progress in commercializing mobile PET systems and compact digital sensor technology. The workshop was expected to become a milestone in advancing interventional radiology, promoting innovation, and strengthening China-Germany collaboration in this frontier area.

2019.5.13

Hosted the 5th INFIERI Summer School on Frontiers in Research and Industry for Intelligent Signal Processing at HUST

The first intelligent signal science exchange meeting of its kind held in Asia.

The fifth INFIERI summer school was organized by the Digital PET Laboratory at Huazhong University of Science and Technology. Its theme was “Integration,” bringing together disciplines and fields and connecting academia with industry. It admitted 50 doctoral students, master’s students, and postdoctoral researchers from universities and research institutes in China, Italy, France, Germany, Spain, Brazil, Argentina, Japan, and other countries. More than 40 experts in high-energy physics, astrophysics, and medical physics from Asia, Europe, and the Americas taught through lectures and experiments. Speakers included Ian Shipsey, Head of Physics at the University of Oxford, and Philip Allport, a member of the European Committee for Future Accelerators’ detector community and Professor of High Energy Physics at the University of Birmingham. INFIERI has a strong international reputation and has previously been held at Oxford, Paris, and Hamburg.
