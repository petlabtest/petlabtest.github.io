Development Philosophy

Several Research Groups

Digital PET Laboratory comprises multiple specialized interdisciplinary research groups, each led by principal investigators with distinctive expertise spanning medical imaging, radiation detection, signal reconstruction, radiopharmacy, and translational clinical research. Rooted in medicine-engineering cross-disciplinary integration, these teams independently advance core technologies of full-digital PET systems while maintaining close internal synergy. Their collective work covers the full innovation chain, including detector chip design, imaging algorithm optimization, prototype system engineering, preclinical animal imaging validation, and clinical translational application. Beyond internal collaboration, each group sustains long-term joint research initiatives with global academic institutions, hospitals and industrial partners, pooling complementary strengths to address bottlenecks in molecular imaging and deliver transformative diagnostic technologies for human health.

Undergraduate Practice Opportunities Program

Lab’s Undergraduate Practice Opportunities Program (UPOP) is a co-curricular professional development program open to HUST sophomores from relevant majors. Whether students aim to pursue careers in industry, academia, or public service, UPOP offers comprehensive guidance throughout the full journey of securing and excelling in their first professional experience, laying a solid foundation for their long-term career growth. We support students in cultivating academic and research competencies, strengthening communication skills, building professional networks, and establishing sustained mentorship connections with industry advisors, enabling them to stand out as they embark on their professional journeys.
