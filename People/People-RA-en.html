RA Development Framework

TALENT DEVELOPMENT

RA at the Core of Talent Development

Grounded in research practice and driven by responsibility, a clear path for progression helps students develop independent thinking, organize collaboration, and advance research outcomes.

The Research Assistant (RA) framework selects and motivates students who demonstrate a strong enthusiasm for academic research, solid practical research skills, a high sense of responsibility and ownership, and a commitment to the laboratory’s vision. It places RA at the core of talent development, encouraging students to take initiative and deliver meaningful work.

The framework comprises six levels, from RA-1 to RA-X. Assessment considers students’ responsibilities, capabilities, and values together, and provides a basis for allocating laboratory resources. A clear path for progression encourages students to strengthen their research skills as they take on responsibility.

GROWTH IN PRACTICE

From Research Practice to Independent Responsibility

Starting with foundational work and modular tasks, students progressively develop capabilities in independent research, collaboration, translation of research outcomes, and mentoring.

Foundational Work and Modular Tasks

Undertake foundational work and modular tasks, developing practical research skills, self-discipline, and responsibility.

Independent Topics and Team Building

Progress toward conceiving research topics independently and forming teams, connecting individual inquiry with collaborative research.

Project Proposals and Core Outcomes

Develop project proposals and produce core research outcomes, turning research ideas into concrete work.

Translation and Mentoring

Advance the translation of research outcomes into industry and mentor the next generation, contributing to a sustained pipeline of research talent.

REVIEW & EVIDENCE

Self-Nomination and Scheduled Review

06Every June12Every December

The framework uses self-nomination, scheduled reviews each June and December, recommendation letters, and verification of supporting work to assess responsibilities, capabilities, and values and guide continued development.

  1. Self-Nomination

    Students participate in RA level assessment through self-nomination.

  2. Scheduled Review

    Reviews take place each year in June and December.

  3. Evidence and Work Verification

    Recommendation letters and verification of supporting work inform the overall assessment.
